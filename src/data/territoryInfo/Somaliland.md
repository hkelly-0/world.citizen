## Info

Somaliland declared independence from Somalia in 1991, as the Somali state collapsed, but few countries recognize it; Israel became the first UN member to do so, in 2025. It was a British protectorate that joined Italian Somalia in 1960, and suffered heavy bombing under Siad Barre in the late 1980s. It has its own government, army, and currency, and has held competitive elections. Most people are Somali Muslims. Livestock exports through Berbera port and remittances drive the economy.
