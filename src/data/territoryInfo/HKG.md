## Info

Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of China. Britain took it after the First Opium War in 1842 and returned it in 1997 under "one country, two systems", which promised wide self-rule until 2047. After huge protests in 2019, a 2020 national security law curbed opposition and free speech. Its 7.4 million people, mostly Cantonese-speaking, live in one of the world's densest cities. A major financial centre and port, it keeps its own currency and legal system.
