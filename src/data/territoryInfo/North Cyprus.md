## More info

Northern Cyprus is a self-declared state recognized only by Turkey; the rest of the world sees it as part of Cyprus. After a 1974 coup backed by Greece's military government, Turkey invaded, and the island has been split along a UN buffer zone ever since. The north declared independence in 1983, and reunification talks have repeatedly failed. Most people are Turkish-speaking Muslims, including many settlers from Turkey. The economy relies on Turkish aid, tourism, and universities.
