## More info

Western Sahara is disputed. Morocco controls most of it and calls it its Southern Provinces; the Polisario Front's Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic, recognized by dozens of countries and an African Union member, claims it all. Spain left in 1975, and war followed until a 1991 ceasefire; the promised independence referendum was never held, and fighting resumed in 2020. Sahrawis speak Hassaniya Arabic, and many live in refugee camps in Algeria. Phosphates and fishing drive the economy.
