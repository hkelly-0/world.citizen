## More info

Western Sahara is disputed. [Morocco]{MAR} controls most of the area, and calls it the Southern Provinces. The Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic (led by the Polisario Front) claims it all, and is recognized by dozens of countries. [Spain]{ESP} left in 1975, and war followed until a 1991 ceasefire. A UN-backed independence referendum was promised but never held, and fighting resumed in 2020. Sahrawis speak Hassaniya Arabic, and were traditionally nomadic herders; many now live in refugee camps in [Algeria]{DZA}.
