## History

The Khmer Empire ruled much of Southeast Asia and built Angkor Wat in the 1100s. Cambodia became a French protectorate in 1863 and gained independence in 1953. The Khmer Rouge ruled from 1975 to 1979 and killed up to two million people. Hun Sen led the country from 1985 to 2023, when his son took over.

## People

Most people are Khmer and Buddhist, with Vietnamese, Chinese, and Cham Muslim minorities. The population is young, partly because of the genocide.

## Economy

Cambodia's economy relies on clothing, tourism, construction, and agriculture. Chinese investment is significant. Online scam centres have become a large industry.

## International Relations

Cambodia is a member of ASEAN and one of China's closest partners in the region. It had a border conflict with Thailand in 2025.
