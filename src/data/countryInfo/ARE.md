## History

The coastal sheikhdoms, known as the Trucial States, were under British protection from the 1800s. Oil was found in the 1950s. In 1971, six emirates united as the United Arab Emirates, and Ras Al Khaimah joined in 1972.

## People

Only about a tenth of residents are Emirati citizens; most are foreign workers, especially from India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. Most live in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Islam is the state religion.

## Economy

The UAE has one of the highest incomes per person in the world. Abu Dhabi has vast oil reserves, while Dubai is a global hub for trade, aviation, tourism, and finance.

## International Relations

The UAE is an influential regional power with close ties to the US, Gulf neighbours, China, and India. It established relations with Israel in 2020 and has been involved in conflicts in Yemen, Libya, and Sudan.
