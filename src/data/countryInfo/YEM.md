## History

Yemen was home to ancient kingdoms, including Saba (Sheba), that grew rich from the incense trade. North Yemen and the British-ruled south developed separately and united in 1990. A civil war began in 2014 when the Houthis seized Sanaa, and a Saudi-led coalition intervened in 2015.

## People

Most Yemenis are Arab and Muslim, split between Sunnis and Zaydi Shias. The population is young and fast-growing. The war has caused one of the world's worst humanitarian crises.

## Economy

Yemen is the poorest country in the Arab world. Its economy, once based on oil, has collapsed during the war, and most people depend on aid.

## International Relations

The Houthis, backed by Iran, control the north, while the internationally recognized government holds the south with Saudi and Emirati support. Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping since 2023 led to US and Israeli strikes.
