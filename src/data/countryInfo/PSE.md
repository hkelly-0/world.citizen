## History

After centuries of Ottoman rule, the land came under British control in 1917. When Israel was founded in 1948, around 700,000 Palestinians fled or were expelled. Israel has occupied the West Bank and Gaza since 1967. The 1993 Oslo Accords created limited Palestinian self-rule, but no state has yet been established.

## People

Palestinians are Arabs, mostly Sunni Muslim with a Christian minority. Millions live as refugees in neighbouring countries. In Gaza, most of the population has been displaced by war since 2023.

## Economy

The Palestinian economy is small and heavily dependent on aid, with Israeli controls on movement and trade. The war has destroyed most of Gaza's economy.

## International Relations

Palestine is recognized as a state by most UN members and is a UN observer state. The West Bank is governed by the Palestinian Authority, while Gaza has been controlled by Hamas since 2007.
