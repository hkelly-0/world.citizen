## History

Nepal was the birthplace of the Buddha. It was united as a kingdom in 1768 and was never colonized. A Maoist insurgency (1996–2006) led to the end of the monarchy in 2008, when Nepal became a republic.

## People

Most people are Hindu, with a Buddhist minority. Nepali is the official language, and over 120 languages are spoken. Mount Everest lies on its border with China.

## Economy

Nepal is one of Asia's poorest countries. Its economy relies on agriculture, tourism, and money sent home by workers in the Gulf, Malaysia, and India.

## International Relations

Nepal balances ties with its two large neighbours, India and China. Its border with India is open, and many Nepalis serve as Gurkhas in the British and Indian armies.
