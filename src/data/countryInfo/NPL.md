## History

Nepal was the birthplace of the Buddha. It was united as a kingdom in 1768 and was never colonized. A Maoist insurgency (1996–2006) led to the end of the monarchy in 2008, when Nepal became a republic.

## People

Most people are Hindu, with a Buddhist minority. Nepali is the official language, and over 120 languages are spoken. Mount Everest lies on its border with [China]{CHN}.

## Economy

Nepal is one of Asia's poorest countries. Its economy relies on agriculture, tourism, and money sent home by workers in the Gulf, [Malaysia]{MYS}, and [India]{IND}.

## International Relations

Nepal balances ties with its two large neighbours, [India]{IND} and [China]{CHN}. Its border with India is open, and many Nepalis serve as Gurkhas in the British and Indian armies.
