## History

The Indigenous Lucayan people were enslaved and wiped out by the Spanish soon after Columbus landed there in 1492. Britain settled the islands in the 1600s, and they were long a haven for pirates. The Bahamas gained independence in 1973.

## People

Most Bahamians are of African descent and speak English. Most people live on New Providence, home to the capital Nassau. Christianity, especially Baptist, plays a large part in public life.

## Economy

The Bahamas is one of the richest countries in the Americas per person. Tourism, much of it from the US and cruise ships, dominates, along with offshore finance. Hurricanes pose a serious threat.

## International Relations

The Bahamas is a member of the Commonwealth and the Caribbean Community (CARICOM), with the British monarch as head of state. Its closest partner is the US, just off its coast.
