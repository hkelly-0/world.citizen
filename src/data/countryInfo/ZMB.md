## History

The region was home to Bantu-speaking kingdoms before the British South Africa Company took control in the 1890s. As Northern Rhodesia, it became a British colony. It gained independence in 1964 under Kenneth Kaunda, who ruled as a one-party state until multiparty elections in 1991. Power has since changed hands peacefully.

## People

Zambia has over 70 ethnic groups, including the Bemba and Tonga. Most people are Christian, and the constitution declares it a Christian nation. English is official, and Bemba and Nyanja are widely spoken.

## Economy

Zambia is one of Africa's largest copper producers, and copper makes up most of its exports. Agriculture employs most people. It defaulted on its debts in 2020 and later restructured them.

## International Relations

Zambia supported liberation movements in neighbouring countries during the struggles against white minority rule. It is a member of the Commonwealth and the Southern African Development Community, and shares Victoria Falls with Zimbabwe.
