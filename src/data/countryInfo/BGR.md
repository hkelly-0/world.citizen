## History

Bulgaria was a powerful medieval empire before being ruled by the Ottomans for nearly five centuries. It became independent in 1878. It was allied with Germany in both World Wars, then was a close Soviet ally until communism ended in 1989.

## People

Most people are ethnic Bulgarians and Orthodox Christians, with Turkish and Roma minorities. Bulgarian is written in Cyrillic. Its population has fallen sharply since 1989.

## Economy

Bulgaria is the EU's poorest member, though incomes have grown. Its economy is based on services, industry, tourism on the Black Sea, and agriculture. It adopted the euro in 2026.

## International Relations

Bulgaria joined NATO in 2004 and the EU in 2007. It has historic and cultural ties with Russia, but supports Western policy on Ukraine.
