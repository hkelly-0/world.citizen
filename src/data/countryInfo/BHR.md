## History

Bahrain was the centre of the ancient Dilmun civilization and later a pearling hub. The Al Khalifa family has ruled since 1783, and it was a British protectorate until independence in 1971. In 2011, protests calling for reform were crushed with help from Saudi troops.

## People

Bahrain is an island country, linked to Saudi Arabia by a causeway. About half of residents are foreign workers. Most citizens are Shia Muslims, while the royal family is Sunni.

## Economy

Bahrain was the first Gulf state to find oil, in 1932, but has smaller reserves than its neighbours. It has developed banking, aluminium, and tourism.

## International Relations

Bahrain is a member of the Gulf Cooperation Council and hosts the US Navy's Fifth Fleet. It established relations with Israel in 2020 and is closely aligned with Saudi Arabia.
