## History

Kyiv was the centre of medieval Kievan Rus'. Ukraine was later divided among Poland-Lithuania and the Russian Empire, and became a Soviet republic. Millions died in a man-made famine, the Holodomor (1932–33). Ukraine became independent in 1991. Russia annexed Crimea in 2014 and launched a full invasion in 2022.

## People

Most people are ethnic Ukrainians, with a large Russian-speaking population, especially in the east. Ukrainian is the official language. Millions have fled the war, many to Poland and Germany.

## Economy

Ukraine has fertile soil and is a major exporter of grain and sunflower oil, as well as metals. The war has caused huge damage, and the economy depends on Western aid.

## International Relations

Ukraine is fighting Russia's invasion with military and financial support from the US and Europe. It is a candidate to join the EU and seeks NATO membership.
