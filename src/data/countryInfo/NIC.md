## History

Nicaragua was colonized by Spain and became independent in 1821. The US occupied it from 1912 to 1933, and the Somoza family ruled as dictators until the Sandinista revolution of 1979. A war followed against the US-backed Contras. Since 2007, the Sandinista leader Daniel Ortega has ruled with growing authoritarianism.

## People

Nicaragua is the largest country in Central America by area. Most people are mestizo and Spanish-speaking, living on the Pacific side. The Caribbean coast has Miskito, Creole, and Garifuna communities, where English and Indigenous languages are spoken.

## Economy

Nicaragua is one of the poorest countries in the Americas. It exports coffee, beef, gold, and textiles, and relies on money sent home by migrants, many of whom work in Costa Rica and the United States.

## International Relations

Nicaragua has tense relations with the US and EU, which have placed sanctions on its government over repression. It is closely aligned with Russia, Cuba, and Venezuela, and switched its recognition from Taiwan to China in 2021.
