## History

The Comoros were part of Swahili trade networks and ruled by sultans. France colonized the islands in the 1800s. Three of them became independent in 1975, while Mayotte voted to stay French. Over 20 coups or attempted coups followed, some led by the French mercenary Bob Denard. Anjouan and Mohéli briefly broke away in 1997.

## People

Most people are of mixed African and Arab descent and Sunni Muslim. Comorian, French, and Arabic are official. Many Comorians try to migrate to Mayotte or France.

## Economy

The Comoros is one of the world's poorest countries. It relies on agriculture, especially vanilla, cloves, and ylang-ylang for perfume, and money sent home by Comorians abroad.

## International Relations

The Comoros claims Mayotte, which remains a French region. It is a member of the Arab League and the African Union.
