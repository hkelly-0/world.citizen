## History

Latvia was ruled for centuries by Germans, Poles, Swedes, and then Russia. It became independent in 1918, but was occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940, when many Latvians were deported. It regained independence in 1991.

## People

About two-thirds of people are ethnic Latvians, and about a quarter are ethnic Russians. Latvian is the only official language. About a third of the population lives in Riga.

## Economy

Latvia has a high-income economy based on services, timber, transport, and food processing. Many young people have emigrated to Western Europe, and the population is shrinking.

## International Relations

Latvia joined the EU and NATO in 2004 and uses the euro. It strongly supports Ukraine and sees Russia as a major security threat.
