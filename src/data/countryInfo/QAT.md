## History

Qatar was a small pearling community ruled by the Al Thani family since the 1800s. It was a British protectorate until independence in 1971. Natural gas, developed from the 1990s, made it one of the world's richest countries.

## People

Only about a tenth of residents are Qatari citizens; most are foreign workers, mainly from South Asia. Most citizens are Sunni Muslims. The treatment of migrant workers drew criticism before the 2022 World Cup.

## Economy

Qatar has one of the world's highest incomes per person, thanks to its huge natural gas reserves. It is a leading exporter of liquefied natural gas and has a large sovereign wealth fund.

## International Relations

Qatar hosts the largest US military base in the Middle East and the news network Al Jazeera. It is a key mediator in conflicts, including Gaza. Its neighbours blockaded it from 2017 to 2021.
