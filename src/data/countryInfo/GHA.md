## History

Ghana was home to the Ashanti Empire, which grew rich from gold. Europeans built forts along the coast for the gold and slave trades. Britain colonized it as the Gold Coast, and in 1957 it became the first sub-Saharan African colony to gain independence, led by Kwame Nkrumah. After several coups, it has been a stable democracy since 1992.

## People

Ghana has many ethnic groups, the largest being the Akan. Most people are Christian, with a Muslim minority in the north. English is official.

## Economy

Ghana is one of Africa's largest gold producers and the world's second-largest cocoa producer, and also exports oil. It defaulted on its debts in 2022 and has since been recovering with IMF support.

## International Relations

Ghana is a member of the Commonwealth and ECOWAS, and is seen as one of Africa's most stable democracies. Nkrumah was a leader of the Pan-African movement.
