## History

Ethiopia is one of the world's oldest countries, home to the ancient Aksumite Empire and an early adopter of Christianity. It defeated Italy in 1896 and was never colonized, apart from Italian occupation in 1936–41. Emperor Haile Selassie was overthrown in 1974. A war in Tigray (2020–22) killed many.

## People

Ethiopia is Africa's second-most populous country, with over 80 ethnic groups. Orthodox Christianity is the largest religion, with many Muslims and Protestants. Amharic is the main working language.

## Economy

Ethiopia's economy has grown quickly, based on agriculture, especially coffee, and services. A huge dam on the Nile provides electricity.

## International Relations

Ethiopia hosts the African Union headquarters in Addis Ababa. It is landlocked and seeks sea access, causing tension with Eritrea and Somalia.
