## History

Armenia was the first country to adopt Christianity as its state religion, in 301. It was later divided between empires, and in 1915 the Ottoman Empire killed around one million Armenians in the Armenian Genocide. It became a Soviet republic and gained independence in 1991.

## People

Almost all people are ethnic Armenians and belong to the Armenian Apostolic Church. Armenian has its own alphabet. Many more Armenians live abroad than in Armenia, especially in Russia, the US, and France.

## Economy

Armenia's economy is based on mining, especially copper, as well as IT, brandy, and money sent home by Armenians abroad. Its borders with Turkey and Azerbaijan are closed.

## International Relations

Armenia fought wars with Azerbaijan over Nagorno-Karabakh, losing the region in 2023, and nearly all its Armenians fled. It has distanced itself from Russia and sought closer ties with the West.
