## History

Carthage, near modern Tunis, was a great ancient power until Rome destroyed it in 146 BC. Tunisia later became Muslim and was part of the Ottoman Empire. France ruled it from 1881 until independence in 1956. Its 2011 revolution sparked the Arab Spring.

## People

Most people are Arab and Sunni Muslim. Arabic is official, and French is widely used. Tunisia has a relatively well-educated population and strong women's rights.

## Economy

Tunisia's economy relies on tourism, agriculture, especially olive oil, phosphates, and manufacturing for Europe. Unemployment is high.

## International Relations

Tunisia has close ties with Europe, especially France and Italy, and many migrants cross from its coast to Europe. Since 2021, its president has taken greater control, drawing criticism.
