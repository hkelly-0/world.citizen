## History

The Kingdom of Kongo and the Teke kingdom ruled the region before France colonized it. Brazzaville was the capital of French Equatorial Africa and of Free France during World War II. It became independent in 1960 and was a Marxist state until 1991. Denis Sassou Nguesso has ruled since 1997, after a civil war, and earlier from 1979 to 1992.

## People

Most people live in Brazzaville or Pointe-Noire. The largest groups are the Kongo, Teke, and Mbochi, and most are Christian. French is official, and Lingala and Kituba are widely spoken.

## Economy

The Republic of the Congo's economy relies heavily on oil, which makes up most of its exports. Timber is also important. Debt is high and many people are poor.

## International Relations

The Republic of the Congo is a member of the African Union and OPEC, and has close ties with France and China. Rainforest covers much of the country.
