## History

Cyprus has been ruled by many powers, including Ottomans and British, and became independent in 1960. Violence between Greek and Turkish Cypriots followed, and in 1974, after a Greek-backed coup, Turkey invaded and occupied the north.

## People

Most people are Greek Cypriots and Orthodox Christians. Turkish Cypriots, mostly Muslim, live in the north, along with many settlers from Turkey. Greek and Turkish are both official.

## Economy

Cyprus has a high-income economy based on tourism, shipping, and financial services. It suffered a banking crisis in 2013. Gas discoveries offshore have raised hopes and tensions.

## International Relations

Cyprus joined the EU in 2004 and uses the euro. The north declared itself a separate state recognized only by Turkey, and UN peacekeepers patrol the buffer zone. Britain keeps two military bases on the island.
