## History

English settlers arrived in Barbados in 1627, and it became a model for sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans across the Caribbean. Slavery was abolished in 1834. Barbados gained independence in 1966 and became a republic in 2021, removing the British monarch as head of state.

## People

Most Barbadians are of African descent and speak English and Bajan Creole. The island is densely populated, with high literacy. The singer Rihanna and cricketer Garfield Sobers are among its famous people.

## Economy

Barbados is one of the wealthier Caribbean countries. Its economy relies on tourism, offshore finance, and rum and sugar exports. Prime Minister Mia Mottley has led global calls to reform climate finance.

## International Relations

Barbados is a member of the Commonwealth and CARICOM. It has led Caribbean calls for reparations for slavery and for richer countries to help small island nations cope with climate change.
