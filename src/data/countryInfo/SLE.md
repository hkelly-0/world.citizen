## History

Britain founded Freetown in 1787 as a home for freed slaves. Sierra Leone became a British colony and gained independence in 1961. A brutal civil war (1991–2002), fuelled by diamonds, killed tens of thousands. An Ebola epidemic struck in 2014–16.

## People

Sierra Leone has many ethnic groups, the largest being the Temne and Mende. Most people are Muslim, with a large Christian minority. English is official, and Krio is widely spoken.

## Economy

Sierra Leone is one of the world's poorest countries. It relies on mining, especially diamonds, iron ore, and rutile, and on agriculture.

## International Relations

Sierra Leone is a member of the Commonwealth and ECOWAS. UN and British troops helped end its civil war.
