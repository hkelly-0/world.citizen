## History

The region was home to the Maravi kingdom and was affected by the Arab-Swahili slave trade. Britain colonized it as Nyasaland in 1891. It became independent in 1964, and Hastings Banda ruled as a dictator until 1994, when it became a multiparty democracy. In 2020, courts overturned a disputed election.

## People

Malawi is one of Africa's most densely populated countries, and most people live in rural areas. Most are Christian, with a Muslim minority. English and Chichewa are widely used. Lake Malawi covers about a fifth of the country.

## Economy

Malawi is one of the world's poorest countries. Most people are farmers, and tobacco, tea, and sugar are its main exports. Droughts and cyclones often cause food shortages.

## International Relations

Malawi is a member of the Commonwealth and the Southern African Development Community, and relies heavily on foreign aid. It is known as "the Warm Heart of Africa".
