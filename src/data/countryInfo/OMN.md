## History

Oman was a seafaring power that ruled Zanzibar and parts of East Africa's coast in the 1700s and 1800s. Sultan Qaboos overthrew his father in 1970 and modernized the country until his death in 2020.

## People

Most citizens are Arab, and most follow Ibadi Islam, a branch distinct from Sunni and Shia. About two-fifths of residents are foreign workers, mainly from South Asia.

## Economy

Oman's economy relies on oil and gas, which are smaller than its neighbours' reserves. It is developing tourism, fishing, logistics, and manufacturing.

## International Relations

Oman follows a neutral foreign policy and is known as a mediator, including between the US and Iran. It is a member of the Gulf Cooperation Council.
