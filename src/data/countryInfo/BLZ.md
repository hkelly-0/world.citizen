## History

Belize was part of the Maya world before British loggers settled the coast in the 1600s. It became the colony of British Honduras in 1862 and gained independence in 1981, with British troops staying to deter Guatemala, which claimed its territory.

## People

Belize has the smallest population in Central America and is very diverse, with Mestizo, Creole, Maya, and Garifuna communities. English is the official language, but Spanish and Kriol are widely spoken.

## Economy

Belize's small economy relies on tourism, especially to the Belize Barrier Reef and Maya ruins, along with exports of sugar, bananas, citrus, and seafood. Public debt is high.

## International Relations

Belize is the only Central American country in the Commonwealth and the Caribbean Community (CARICOM), with the British monarch as head of state. Guatemala's territorial claim is being decided by the International Court of Justice.
