## History

Norway was a Viking homeland and later united with Denmark for over four centuries. It was handed to Sweden in 1814 and became fully independent in 1905. Germany occupied it during World War II. Oil discovered in the North Sea in 1969 transformed the country.

## People

Most people are ethnic Norwegians and speak Norwegian, which has two written forms. The Indigenous Sámi live mostly in the north. Most of the population lives in the south, around Oslo and along the coast.

## Economy

Norway is one of the richest countries in the world, thanks to oil and gas, as well as fishing and shipping. Its oil wealth is saved in the world's largest sovereign wealth fund. Most new cars sold are electric.

## International Relations

Norway is a founding member of NATO but has twice voted against joining the EU, instead taking part in the EU single market. It is known for peace mediation and awards the Nobel Peace Prize.
