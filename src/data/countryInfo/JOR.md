## History

Jordan was home to the Nabataeans, who carved the city of Petra. After Ottoman and British rule, it became independent in 1946 under the Hashemite monarchy. It lost the West Bank to Israel in 1967.

## People

Most Jordanians are Arab and Sunni Muslim, and many are of Palestinian origin. It has taken in large numbers of refugees from Palestine, Iraq, and Syria. Most people live in and around Amman.

## Economy

Jordan has few natural resources and very little water. Its economy relies on services, tourism, phosphates, and foreign aid, especially from the US and Gulf states.

## International Relations

Jordan is a close ally of the US and made peace with Israel in 1994. It acts as custodian of the Muslim and Christian holy sites in Jerusalem.
