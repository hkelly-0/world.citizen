## History

The Pope ruled the Papal States across central Italy for over one thousand years until they were taken over during Italy's unification in 1870. Vatican City was created in 1929 by a treaty with Italy, giving the Pope a sovereign territory.

## People

Vatican City is the world's smallest country, with only a few hundred residents, mostly clergy and the Swiss Guard. Italian and Latin are both used. The Pope is head of state.

## Economy

The Vatican is funded by donations from Catholics worldwide, museum tickets, investments, and the sale of stamps, coins, and souvenirs. It uses the euro.

## International Relations

The Holy See, the Pope's government, has diplomatic relations with most countries and is an observer at the UN. It leads the Catholic Church, which has well over a billion members worldwide.
