## History

Togo became a German colony in 1884, then was divided between Britain and France after World War I. The British part joined Ghana, and French Togoland became independent in 1960. Gnassingbé Eyadéma seized power in 1967, and his son Faure Gnassingbé succeeded him in 2005. A new constitution in 2024 created a new top post without term limits.

## People

Togo has about 40 ethnic groups, including the Ewe and Kabye. Christianity, Islam, and traditional religions, including Vodun, are all practiced. French is official.

## Economy

Togo's economy relies on agriculture, phosphates, and the deep-water port of Lomé, one of West Africa's busiest, which serves landlocked neighbours.

## International Relations

Togo is a member of ECOWAS and joined the Commonwealth in 2022, despite having no British colonial history. It faces jihadist attacks in its north from the Sahel.
