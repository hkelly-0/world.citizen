## History

Equatorial Guinea was a Spanish colony and became independent in 1968. Its first president, Francisco Macías Nguema, ruled brutally, and up to a third of the population was killed or fled. His nephew, Teodoro Obiang, overthrew him in 1979 and has ruled ever since, making him the world's longest-serving president.

## People

Equatorial Guinea is the only African country with Spanish as an official language. Most people are Fang and Catholic. The capital, Malabo, is on Bioko island, while most people live on the mainland.

## Economy

Oil and gas made Equatorial Guinea one of Africa's richest countries per person, but most people remain poor. Production has declined.

## International Relations

Equatorial Guinea is a member of the African Union and OPEC. It is criticized for corruption and human rights abuses.
