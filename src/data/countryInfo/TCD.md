## History

Chad was home to the Kanem-Bornu Empire, which controlled trade across the Sahara. France colonized it, and it became independent in 1960. Civil wars followed, along with a war with Libya over the Aouzou Strip in the 1980s. Idriss Déby ruled from 1990 until his death in 2021, and his son Mahamat took power.

## People

Chad has over 200 ethnic groups and is split between a mostly Muslim north and a mostly Christian south. Arabic and French are official. It hosts many refugees from Sudan.

## Economy

Chad is one of the world's poorest countries. Oil is its main export, while most people rely on farming and herding. Lake Chad has shrunk dramatically.

## International Relations

Chad ended its military cooperation with France in 2024, and French troops left. It hosts refugees from Sudan's war.
