## History

Sweden was a Viking homeland and became a great power in the 1600s, ruling much of the Baltic region. It lost Finland to Russia in 1809 and has not fought a war since 1814. It stayed neutral in both World Wars, and built a large welfare state in the 1900s.

## People

Most people are ethnic Swedes and speak Swedish, with Sámi and Finnish minorities. About one in five people were born abroad, notably in Syria, Iraq, and Finland. Most people live in the south.

## Economy

Sweden has a wealthy economy with a large welfare state. It is home to global companies such as Volvo, IKEA, Ericsson, and Spotify. Major exports include machinery, vehicles, timber, and iron ore.

## International Relations

Sweden was neutral for two centuries but joined the EU in 1995 and NATO in 2024, after Russia's invasion of Ukraine. It has long been a large aid donor and a supporter of the UN.
