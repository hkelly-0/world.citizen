## History

Uzbekistan's cities, including Samarkand and Bukhara, were major stops on the Silk Road. Timur (Tamerlane) built an empire from Samarkand in the 1300s. Russia conquered the region in the 1800s, and it became a Soviet republic. It gained independence in 1991 and was ruled by Islam Karimov until 2016.

## People

Uzbekistan is Central Asia's most populous country. Most people are ethnic Uzbeks and Sunni Muslims, with Tajik, Kazakh, and Russian minorities. Uzbek is official, and Russian is widely used.

## Economy

Uzbekistan's economy relies on gold, natural gas, cotton, and money sent home by migrants, mainly from Russia. Reforms since 2016 have opened the economy.

## International Relations

Uzbekistan follows a balanced foreign policy, with close ties to Russia, China, and its Central Asian neighbours. It is doubly landlocked, surrounded by other landlocked countries.
