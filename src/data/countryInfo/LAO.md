## History

Laos grew from the kingdom of Lan Xang, founded in 1353. It became part of French Indochina and gained independence in 1953. During the Vietnam War, the US dropped more bombs on Laos than on Germany and Japan in World War II. The communist Pathet Lao took power in 1975.

## People

Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia. Most people are Lao and Buddhist, with many ethnic minorities such as the Hmong. Most live in rural areas.

## Economy

Laos has a communist government and a mostly agricultural economy. It sells hydropower to its neighbours and has taken on large debts to China, which built a high-speed railway.

## International Relations

Laos is a member of ASEAN and has close ties with Vietnam and China. Unexploded bombs still kill and injure people.
