## History

Kazakhstan's steppe was home to nomadic peoples for millennia. It came under Russian rule in the 1700s and 1800s and became a Soviet republic, used for nuclear tests and forced resettlement. A famine in the 1930s killed over a third of Kazakhs. It became independent in 1991.

## People

Kazakhstan is the world's largest landlocked country. Most people are Kazakhs, with a large Russian minority, especially in the north. Kazakh and Russian are both used, and most people are Muslim.

## Economy

Kazakhstan has Central Asia's largest economy, based on oil, gas, uranium, and metals. It is the world's largest uranium producer.

## International Relations

Kazakhstan balances ties with Russia, China, and the West. It is part of the Eurasian Economic Union, and hosts the Baikonur Cosmodrome, which Russia leases for space launches.
