## History

Tajikistan's people descend from the Persian-speaking peoples of Central Asia. Russia conquered the region in the 1800s, and it became a Soviet republic. After independence in 1991, a civil war (1992–97) killed tens of thousands. Emomali Rahmon has ruled since 1992.

## People

Most people are Tajiks, who speak a form of Persian, and most are Sunni Muslims. Mountains, including the Pamirs, cover over 90% of the country.

## Economy

Tajikistan is the poorest of the former Soviet republics. Money sent home by migrants, mainly working in Russia, makes up a large share of its income. It also exports aluminium and cotton, and has huge hydropower potential.

## International Relations

Tajikistan relies on Russia, which has a military base there, for security, and on China for investment. It shares a long border with Afghanistan.
