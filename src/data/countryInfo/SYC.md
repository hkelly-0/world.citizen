## History

Seychelles was uninhabited until the French settled it in 1770, bringing enslaved Africans. Britain took control in 1814. It became independent in 1976 and was a one-party state from 1977 until 1993.

## People

Seychelles has Africa's smallest population. Most people are of mixed African, European, and Asian descent and speak Seychellois Creole. Most are Catholic.

## Economy

Seychelles has Africa's highest income per person, based on tourism and tuna fishing. Much of its land and sea is protected.

## International Relations

Seychelles is a member of the Commonwealth and the African Union. It has worked with other countries to fight piracy, and India and China compete for influence there.
