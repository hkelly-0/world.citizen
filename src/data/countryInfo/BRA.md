## History

Brazil was colonized by Portugal from 1500, and about four million enslaved Africans were brought to work its plantations and mines. It became independent in 1822, was the last country in the Americas to abolish slavery, in 1888, and became a republic in 1889. A military dictatorship ruled from 1964 to 1985.

## People

Brazil is the largest country in Latin America, and most people are of mixed, European, or African descent. Portuguese is the language. It has the world's largest Catholic population, but evangelical Protestantism is growing fast.

## Economy

Brazil has the largest economy in Latin America. It is a major exporter of soybeans, iron ore, oil, coffee, sugar, and beef, with large industrial and services sectors. Deforestation of the Amazon is a major concern.

## International Relations

Brazil is a founding member of BRICS and Mercosur, and belongs to the G20. It is a leading voice for developing countries. The Amazon rainforest, most of which lies in Brazil, is central to global climate efforts.
