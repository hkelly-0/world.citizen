## History

Benin was home to the Kingdom of Dahomey, which grew rich from the slave trade and was known for its female warriors. France colonized it, and it became independent in 1960 as Dahomey. After several coups, it was a Marxist state from 1975 to 1990, then became one of the first African countries to make a peaceful transition to democracy.

## People

Benin has many ethnic groups, including the Fon and Yoruba. Christianity, Islam, and Vodun, which originated here, are all practiced. French is official.

## Economy

Benin's economy relies on cotton, cashews, and trade through the port of Cotonou, much of it to Nigeria.

## International Relations

Benin is a member of ECOWAS. Jihadist violence has spread into its north from the Sahel. France has returned some royal treasures looted from Dahomey.
