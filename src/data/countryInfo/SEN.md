## History

Senegal was part of several West African empires. Gorée Island became a symbol of the Atlantic slave trade. France colonized the region, and Dakar was the capital of French West Africa. Senegal became independent in 1960 under the poet Léopold Senghor. It has never had a military coup, and power has changed hands peacefully.

## People

Most people are Sunni Muslim, with Sufi brotherhoods playing a big role in daily life and politics. The largest groups are the Wolof, Fula, and Serer. Wolof is widely spoken, and French is official.

## Economy

Senegal's economy relies on agriculture, fishing, phosphates, and services. Offshore oil and gas production began in 2024, though hidden debts have caused a financial crisis.

## International Relations

Senegal is a member of ECOWAS and is known as one of Africa's most stable democracies. At its request, French troops left the country in 2025.
