## History

Portugal has had largely the same borders since the 1200s. It led the Age of Discovery, building an empire in Brazil, Africa, and Asia. A dictatorship ruled from 1926 until the Carnation Revolution of 1974, after which its African colonies became independent.

## People

Most people speak Portuguese and are Catholic. Many have roots in former colonies such as Brazil, Angola, and Cape Verde. Millions of Portuguese live abroad.

## Economy

Portugal's economy is based on tourism, services, textiles, cork, wine, and cars. It needed a bailout in the 2011 eurozone crisis but has since recovered. Most of its electricity comes from renewable sources.

## International Relations

Portugal is a founding member of NATO and joined the EU in 1986. Its alliance with England, dating from 1386, is the world's oldest still in force. It leads the Community of Portuguese Language Countries.
