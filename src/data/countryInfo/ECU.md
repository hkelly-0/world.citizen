## History

Ecuador was part of the Inca Empire before Spain conquered it in the 1530s. It won independence in 1822 as part of Gran Colombia and became a separate country in 1830. Its history has been marked by political instability, including a border war with Peru that ended in 1998.

## People

Most Ecuadorians are mestizo, with Indigenous peoples, including Kichwa speakers, and Afro-Ecuadorian minorities. Spanish is dominant. The country spans the Andes, the Amazon, the Pacific coast, and the Galápagos Islands.

## Economy

Ecuador's economy relies on oil, bananas, shrimp, cut flowers, and cocoa. It has used the US dollar since 2000. In recent years, drug gangs have caused a sharp rise in violent crime.

## International Relations

Ecuador is a member of the Andean Community, and its main trading partners are the US and China. It became the first country to give legal rights to nature in its 2008 constitution.
