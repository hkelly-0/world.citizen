## History

The Māori settled New Zealand around 1300. Britain made it a colony after the Treaty of Waitangi with Māori chiefs in 1840, followed by land wars. It was the first country to give women the vote, in 1893, and gradually became independent.

## People

Most people are of European descent, with large Māori, Asian, and Pacific Islander communities. English, Māori, and New Zealand Sign Language are official. Most people live on the North Island.

## Economy

New Zealand has a wealthy economy, based on agriculture, especially dairy and meat, tourism, and services.

## International Relations

New Zealand is a member of the Commonwealth and has close ties with Australia and the Pacific Islands. It has been nuclear-free since 1987.
