## History

Palau's islands have been inhabited for thousands of years. Spain claimed them, then sold them to Germany in 1899. Japan took control in 1914, and the islands saw fierce fighting in World War II, especially on Peleliu. The US then governed them, and Palau became independent in 1994 under a Compact of Free Association with the US.

## People

Palau has one of the world's smallest populations. Most people are Palauan and Christian. Palauan and English are official, and many foreign workers come from the Philippines.

## Economy

Palau's economy relies on tourism, especially diving, and US aid. It protects most of its waters in a marine sanctuary.

## International Relations

Under its compact, the US provides defence and aid, and Palauans can live and work in the US. Palau is one of the few countries that recognizes Taiwan instead of China.
