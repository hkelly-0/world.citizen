## History

Palau's islands have been inhabited for thousands of years. [Spain]{ESP} claimed them, then sold them to [Germany]{DEU} in 1899. [Japan]{JPN} took control in 1914, and the islands saw fierce fighting in World War II, especially on Peleliu. The [US]{USA} then governed them, and Palau became independent in 1994 under a Compact of Free Association with the US.

## People

Palau has one of the world's smallest populations. Most people are Palauan and Christian. Palauan and English are official, and many foreign workers come from the [Philippines]{PHL}.

## Economy

Palau's economy relies on tourism, especially diving, and [US]{USA} aid. It protects most of its waters in a marine sanctuary.

## International Relations

Under its compact, the [US]{USA} provides defence and aid, and Palauans can live and work in the US. Palau is one of the few countries that recognizes [Taiwan]{TWN} instead of [China]{CHN}.
