## History

The islands have been inhabited for thousands of years, and Pohnpei is home to the ancient stone city of Nan Madol. Spain, Germany, and Japan controlled them, and the US governed them after World War II. The Federated States of Micronesia became independent in 1986 under a Compact of Free Association with the US.

## People

The country is made up of four states, Yap, Chuuk, Pohnpei, and Kosrae, spread over hundreds of islands, each with its own language and culture. Most people are Christian. Many have moved to the US.

## Economy

The economy relies on US aid, fishing licences, and subsistence farming.

## International Relations

Under its compact, the US provides defence and aid, and citizens can live and work in the US. Like other Pacific islands, it is threatened by rising seas.
