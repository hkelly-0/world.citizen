## History

Rome built an empire across the Mediterranean, and later Italy was the birthplace of the Renaissance. Divided for centuries, it was united in 1861. Benito Mussolini's Fascists ruled from 1922 and allied with Nazi Germany in World War II. Italy became a republic in 1946.

## People

Most Italians speak Italian, with many regional dialects, and most are Catholic. The population is ageing and shrinking. The north is wealthier than the south, and millions of Italians emigrated in the past.

## Economy

Italy has one of the largest economies in Europe, with strong manufacturing in machinery, cars, fashion, and food, as well as tourism. Public debt is high, and growth has been slow for decades.

## International Relations

Italy is a founding member of the EU and NATO, and a member of the G7. As a Mediterranean country, it is a main entry point for migrants crossing from Africa.
