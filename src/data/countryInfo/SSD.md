## History

South Sudan was ruled with Sudan by Britain and Egypt, and the south was largely neglected. After Sudan's independence in 1956, two long civil wars killed millions. A 2011 referendum led to independence, making it the world's newest country. A civil war began in 2013 between factions of the ruling party, and a 2018 peace deal remains fragile.

## People

South Sudan has many ethnic groups, the largest being the Dinka and Nuer. Most people are Christian or follow traditional religions. English is official. Millions have been displaced by conflict and flooding.

## Economy

South Sudan is one of the world's poorest countries. Oil provides almost all government income, exported by pipeline through Sudan. Many people rely on cattle herding and food aid.

## International Relations

South Sudan relies on international aid and UN peacekeepers. Sudan's war has disrupted its oil exports and sent refugees across the border.
