## History

Romania was formed in 1859 when Wallachia and Moldavia united, and it became independent from the Ottomans in 1878. It gained Transylvania after World War I. Under communism, Nicolae Ceaușescu ruled harshly until he was overthrown and executed in 1989.

## People

Most people are ethnic Romanians and speak Romanian, a Latin language, with Hungarian and Roma minorities. Most are Orthodox Christians. Millions of Romanians work in Western Europe.

## Economy

Romania's economy has grown quickly since joining the EU, led by IT, car manufacturing, and agriculture. It also has oil and gas resources.

## International Relations

Romania joined NATO in 2004 and the EU in 2007. It hosts NATO forces and supports Ukraine and Moldova against Russia.
