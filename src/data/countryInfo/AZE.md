## History

Azerbaijan was ruled by Persian, Turkic, and Russian powers, and Baku became a centre of the world's oil industry around 1900. It became a Soviet republic and gained independence in 1991. It lost control of Nagorno-Karabakh in a war with Armenia, then retook it in 2020 and 2023.

## People

Most people are ethnic Azerbaijanis and speak Azerbaijani, a Turkic language. Most are Shia Muslims, though the country is secular. Many more Azerbaijanis live in neighbouring Iran.

## Economy

Azerbaijan's economy depends heavily on oil and gas, which make up most of its exports, piped to Europe through Georgia and Turkey.

## International Relations

Azerbaijan is a close ally of Turkey and an important gas supplier to Europe. It has been ruled by the Aliyev family since 1993, and is criticized for repressing opponents.
