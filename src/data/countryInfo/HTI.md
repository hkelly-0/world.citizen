## History

Haiti was France's richest colony, built on the labour of enslaved Africans. In 1804, after a successful slave revolt, it became the first Black republic, but was forced to pay France a huge indemnity. It was occupied by the US (1915–34), ruled by the Duvalier dictatorship, and devastated by an earthquake in 2010.

## People

Almost all Haitians are of African descent. Haitian Creole is spoken by everyone, and French by an educated minority. Catholicism and Vodou are both widely practiced. Many Haitians live abroad, especially in the US.

## Economy

Haiti is the poorest country in the Americas. Most people work in farming or informal trade, and money sent home by migrants is vital. Gang violence, political crisis, and natural disasters have badly damaged the economy.

## International Relations

Haiti has received large amounts of foreign aid and several UN missions. Since 2024, a Kenyan-led international force has supported its police against armed gangs. Relations with the neighbouring Dominican Republic are strained over migration.
