## History

Guatemala was a heartland of the Maya civilization, with cities like Tikal. Spain conquered the region in the 1520s, and it gained independence in 1821. In 1954 a US-backed coup overthrew its elected president, leading to a civil war (1960–96) in which around 200,000 people were killed, mostly Indigenous Maya.

## People

Guatemala is Central America's most populous country. Around 40% of people are Indigenous, mostly Maya, speaking over 20 Mayan languages alongside Spanish. Most people are Catholic or evangelical Protestant.

## Economy

Guatemala has the largest economy in Central America, based on agriculture (coffee, sugar, bananas, cardamom), textiles, and services. Money sent home by migrants in the US is vital. Poverty and inequality are high, especially among Indigenous people.

## International Relations

Guatemala is a member of the Central American Integration System and has free trade with the US. It has a long-standing claim to much of neighbouring Belize. It is one of the few countries that recognizes Taiwan instead of China.
