## History

Venezuela was colonized by Spain and won independence in 1821, led by Simón Bolívar, who was born in Caracas. Oil was discovered in the early 1900s, bringing great wealth. Hugo Chávez was elected in 1998 and launched a socialist "Bolivarian Revolution", continued by Nicolás Maduro from 2013 amid economic collapse.

## People

Most Venezuelans are mestizo and speak Spanish, and most live in northern cities. Since 2014, around eight million people have left, one of the largest displacement crises in the world.

## Economy

Venezuela has the world's largest proven oil reserves, which provide almost all its exports. Mismanagement, falling oil prices, and US sanctions caused a deep collapse and hyperinflation after 2013, though the economy has partly recovered.

## International Relations

Venezuela's government is in conflict with the US, which has imposed sanctions. Many countries disputed Maduro's 2024 re-election. It is allied with Cuba, Russia, China, and Iran, and claims much of neighbouring Guyana.
