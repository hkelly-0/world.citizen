## History

Liberia was founded in 1822 by the American Colonization Society as a home for freed African Americans, who dominated the Indigenous population. It became independent in 1847, making it Africa's oldest republic. Two civil wars (1989–2003) killed about 250,000 people. Ellen Johnson Sirleaf became Africa's first elected female head of state in 2006.

## People

Liberia has many ethnic groups, including the Kpelle and Bassa, and a small Americo-Liberian community. Most people are Christian, with a Muslim minority. English is official.

## Economy

Liberia is one of the world's poorest countries. It relies on iron ore, rubber, gold, and timber, and has one of the world's largest ship registries. An Ebola epidemic in 2014–16 hit the economy hard.

## International Relations

Liberia has close historical ties with the US, and used the US dollar alongside its own currency. It is a member of ECOWAS.
