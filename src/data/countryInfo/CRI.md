## History

Costa Rica was a sparsely settled Spanish colony and became independent in 1821. It has been one of Latin America's most stable democracies. After a brief civil war in 1948, it abolished its army and spent the money on education and healthcare instead.

## People

Most Costa Ricans are of mixed or European descent and speak Spanish, with an Afro-Caribbean community on the Caribbean coast. Many immigrants come from Nicaragua. Life expectancy is among the highest in the Americas.

## Economy

Costa Rica has an upper-middle-income economy. Medical devices, tourism, and services lead, alongside coffee, bananas, and pineapples. Almost all its electricity comes from renewable sources, and much of its land is protected forest.

## International Relations

Without an army, Costa Rica relies on international law and diplomacy and is known for promoting peace, human rights, and environmental protection. It hosts the Inter-American Court of Human Rights and has close ties with the United States.
