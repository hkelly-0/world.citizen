## History

Gabon was home to Bantu-speaking peoples and became a French colony. It became independent in 1960. Omar Bongo ruled from 1967 until his death in 2009, and his son Ali Bongo succeeded him until a military coup in 2023. The coup leader, Brice Oligui Nguema, was elected president in 2025.

## People

Gabon has a small population, most of whom live in cities, especially Libreville. The largest group is the Fang, and most people are Christian. French is official.

## Economy

Gabon is one of Africa's wealthier countries per person, relying on oil, manganese, and timber, though many people are poor. Rainforest covers most of the country, and it protects many forest elephants.

## International Relations

Gabon has close ties with France and joined the Commonwealth in 2022, despite having no British colonial history.
