## History

Spain colonized Trinidad, and Britain took it in 1797, later joining it with Tobago. After slavery ended in 1834, hundreds of thousands of indentured labourers were brought from India. The country gained independence in 1962 and became a republic in 1976.

## People

The population is roughly equally split between people of Indian and African descent, with many of mixed heritage. English is official. Religions include Christianity, Hinduism, and Islam. It is the birthplace of calypso, soca, and the steelpan.

## Economy

Trinidad and Tobago is one of the richest Caribbean countries, thanks to oil and natural gas, which it exports as liquefied gas, ammonia, and methanol. Falling gas output is a challenge.

## International Relations

Trinidad and Tobago is a member of the Commonwealth and CARICOM, which is headquartered nearby in Guyana. It hosts the Caribbean Court of Justice. It sits just off Venezuela's coast, with which it has energy and migration links.
