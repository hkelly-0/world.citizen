## History

Slovenia was ruled by the Habsburgs for centuries, then joined Yugoslavia after World War I. It was the first republic to break away, declaring independence in 1991 after a ten-day war with very few deaths.

## People

Most people are ethnic Slovenes and speak Slovene, with small Italian and Hungarian minorities. Most are Catholic. Forests cover more than half the country.

## Economy

Slovenia is the richest of the former Yugoslav republics, with strong exports of cars, pharmaceuticals, and electrical goods. Koper is an important port.

## International Relations

Slovenia joined the EU and NATO in 2004, and was the first former communist country to adopt the euro, in 2007. It has close ties with Austria, Italy, and Croatia.
