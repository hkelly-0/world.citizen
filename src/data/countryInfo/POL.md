## History

Poland, united with Lithuania, was once one of Europe's largest countries, but was partitioned by its neighbours and disappeared from the map in 1795. It regained independence in 1918. Germany invaded in 1939, starting World War II, and millions of Poles, including most of its Jews, were killed. Communist rule ended in 1989.

## People

Most Poles are ethnic Poles and Catholic, and Poland is one of Europe's most religious countries. Millions of Ukrainians have arrived since 2022. Many Poles have moved to Western Europe.

## Economy

Poland has one of Europe's fastest-growing economies since 1989, with strong manufacturing, services, and agriculture. It is the largest economy in Central Europe.

## International Relations

Poland joined NATO in 1999 and the EU in 2004. It is one of Ukraine's strongest supporters and spends a large share of its income on defence, seeing Russia as a major threat.
