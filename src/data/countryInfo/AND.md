## History

Andorra has been a co-principality since 1278, shared between the Bishop of Urgell in Spain and, today, the President of France. It adopted its first constitution in 1993.

## People

Andorra is a small mountain country between France and Spain. Catalan is the official language, and Spanish, French, and Portuguese are widely spoken. Most residents are foreign citizens.

## Economy

Andorra's economy is based on tourism, especially skiing, and tax-free shopping. Low taxes have made it attractive for wealthy residents. It uses the euro.

## International Relations

Andorra is not in the EU but has close agreements with it. France and Spain are responsible for its defence.
