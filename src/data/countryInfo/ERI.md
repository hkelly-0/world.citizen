## History

Eritrea's coast was part of the ancient Aksumite Empire. Italy colonized it from 1890, then Britain ran it after World War II, and it was joined to Ethiopia in 1952. After a 30-year war, it became independent in 1993. A border war with Ethiopia (1998–2000) killed tens of thousands. Isaias Afwerki has ruled since independence.

## People

Eritrea's population is roughly split between Christians and Muslims, with nine ethnic groups, the largest being the Tigrinya. Many young people have fled indefinite military service.

## Economy

Eritrea is one of Africa's poorest countries. Most people rely on farming and herding, and it exports gold, copper, and zinc. Its economy is largely controlled by the state.

## International Relations

Eritrea is one of the world's most closed countries, with no elections or free press. It made peace with Ethiopia in 2018, but relations have become tense again.
