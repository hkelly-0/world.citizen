## History

Nigeria was home to kingdoms such as Oyo, Benin, and the Sokoto Caliphate. Britain colonized it, and it became independent in 1960. A civil war (1967–70) over Biafra killed about one million people. After years of military rule, it became a democracy in 1999.

## People

Nigeria is Africa's most populous country, with over 250 ethnic groups, including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo. It is split between a mostly Muslim north and a mostly Christian south. English is official.

## Economy

Nigeria has one of Africa's largest economies, relying on oil and gas, agriculture, and services. Its film industry, Nollywood, and music are popular across Africa.

## International Relations

Nigeria is a member of the Commonwealth and ECOWAS, and a leading regional power. It is fighting jihadist groups such as Boko Haram in the northeast.
