## History

Switzerland began as an alliance of Alpine cantons in 1291, gradually expanding. It has been neutral since 1815 and stayed out of both World Wars. Its modern federal state was created in 1848. It practices direct democracy, with frequent national referendums.

## People

Switzerland has four national languages: German, French, Italian, and Romansh. About a quarter of residents are foreign citizens. Most people are Christian, though many are non-religious.

## Economy

Switzerland is one of the richest countries in the world. It is known for banking, pharmaceuticals, watches, machinery, and chocolate. Companies like Nestlé, Roche, and Novartis are based there.

## International Relations

Switzerland is neutral and not in the EU or NATO, but has many agreements with the EU. Geneva hosts the UN's European headquarters, the Red Cross, and many other international organizations.
