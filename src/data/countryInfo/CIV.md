## History

Côte d'Ivoire was a French colony and became independent in 1960. Under Félix Houphouët-Boigny, who ruled until 1993, it was one of West Africa's most prosperous countries. Disputes over national identity led to civil wars in 2002–07 and 2010–11, when Laurent Gbagbo refused to accept his election defeat.

## People

Côte d'Ivoire has over 60 ethnic groups and is split between a mostly Muslim north and a mostly Christian south. Many people have roots in Burkina Faso and Mali. French is official. Abidjan is the largest city, while Yamoussoukro is the capital.

## Economy

Côte d'Ivoire is the world's largest cocoa producer and one of Africa's fastest-growing economies. It also exports cashews, oil, gold, and rubber.

## International Relations

Côte d'Ivoire is a member of ECOWAS and has close ties with France, whose base in Abidjan was handed over in 2025.
