## History

Russia grew from medieval Kievan Rus' into a vast empire under the tsars, expanding across Siberia to the Pacific. The 1917 revolution created the Soviet Union, which lost tens of millions in World War II and became a superpower. After it collapsed in 1991, Russia became independent, and Vladimir Putin has led it since 2000.

## People

Russia is the largest country in the world by area. Most people are ethnic Russians and Orthodox Christians, with nearly 200 ethnic groups, including Tatars and Chechens. Most people live in the European west.

## Economy

Russia's economy relies heavily on oil, gas, metals, and grain exports. Since its invasion of Ukraine in 2022, it has faced heavy Western sanctions and shifted trade towards China and India.

## International Relations

Russia is a nuclear power and a permanent member of the UN Security Council. Its annexation of Crimea in 2014 and full invasion of Ukraine in 2022 caused a deep rift with the West. It is a founding member of BRICS.
