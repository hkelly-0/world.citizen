## History

Indonesia's islands were home to Hindu and Buddhist kingdoms, and later Muslim sultanates. The Dutch colonized the islands over several centuries. Indonesia declared independence in 1945 and won it after a war in 1949. Suharto ruled as a dictator from 1967 to 1998, after anti-communist killings in 1965–66.

## People

Indonesia is the world's fourth-most populous country and has the world's largest Muslim population. It has hundreds of ethnic groups, with Indonesian as the national language. Over half of people live on Java.

## Economy

Indonesia has Southeast Asia's largest economy, based on coal, palm oil, nickel, manufacturing, and services. It is building a new capital, Nusantara, on Borneo.

## International Relations

Indonesia is a founding member of ASEAN and a member of the G20 and BRICS. It follows a non-aligned foreign policy and is an influential voice in the Muslim world.
