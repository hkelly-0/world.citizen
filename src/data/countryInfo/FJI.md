## History

Fiji was settled about 3,000 years ago. Britain made it a colony in 1874 and brought indentured labourers from India to work on sugar plantations. It became independent in 1970. Tensions between Indigenous and Indo-Fijians led to several coups between 1987 and 2006.

## People

Most people are Indigenous Fijians, mostly Christian, with a large Indo-Fijian minority, mostly Hindu. English, Fijian, and Hindi are official.

## Economy

Fiji has one of the Pacific's largest economies, based on tourism, sugar, bottled water, and money sent home by Fijians abroad.

## International Relations

Fiji is a member of the Commonwealth and the Pacific Islands Forum, which has its headquarters in Suva. Its soldiers serve in many UN peacekeeping missions.
