## History

German states were united in 1871. Germany was defeated in World War I, and under the Nazis started World War II and carried out the Holocaust, murdering six million Jews. After defeat it was divided into West and East Germany, which reunited in 1990 after the fall of the Berlin Wall.

## People

Germany is the EU's most populous country. Most people speak German, and many have roots in Turkey, Poland, Syria, and other countries. Religion is split between Catholics, Protestants, and a growing non-religious majority.

## Economy

Germany has Europe's largest economy, built on engineering, cars, chemicals, and machinery. Companies such as Volkswagen, Siemens, and BASF are world leaders. It is one of the world's biggest exporters.

## International Relations

Germany is a founding member of the EU and a member of NATO and the G7. After World War II, it favoured diplomacy over military power, but has expanded its defences since Russia's invasion of Ukraine.
