## History

Saint Lucia's Indigenous Kalinago resisted early European settlement. France and Britain fought over the island, which changed hands fourteen times before Britain took final control in 1814. Sugar plantations used enslaved African labour until 1834. Saint Lucia gained independence in 1979.

## People

Most Saint Lucians are of African descent. English is official, but many speak Kwéyòl, a French-based Creole. Most are Catholic. The island has produced two Nobel laureates, Derek Walcott and Arthur Lewis.

## Economy

Tourism is the mainstay, drawn by beaches and the volcanic Pitons. Bananas were once the main export. The country also sells citizenship to foreign investors.

## International Relations

Saint Lucia is a member of the Commonwealth, CARICOM, and the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States, which is based in its capital. The British monarch is head of state. It recognizes Taiwan instead of China.
