## History

Cape Verde was uninhabited until the Portuguese settled it in the 1400s, using it as a hub for the slave trade. Droughts and famine killed many people over the centuries. It fought for independence alongside Guinea-Bissau and became independent in 1975, and has been a multiparty democracy since 1991.

## People

Cape Verde is made up of ten volcanic islands. Most people are of mixed African and European descent. Portuguese is official, and Cape Verdean Creole is widely spoken. More Cape Verdeans live abroad than at home, especially in the US and Portugal.

## Economy

Cape Verde has few natural resources and little rain. Its economy relies on tourism and money sent home by Cape Verdeans abroad.

## International Relations

Cape Verde is a member of ECOWAS and the Community of Portuguese Language Countries. It is one of Africa's most stable democracies.
