## History

England, Wales, Scotland, and Ireland were gradually united over several centuries, although most of Ireland became independent in 1922. At its height in 1920, Britain ruled the largest empire in history. After World War II, most of its colonies gained independence, and many joined the Commonwealth. More recently, the UK left the European Union in 2020.

## People

Most of the UK's people live in England, and London is by far the largest city. The country is increasingly diverse: around 16% are foreign-born, notably from India, Poland, and Pakistan. English is dominant; Welsh and Scottish Gaelic are also recognized.

## Economy

The UK has an advanced, services-led economy, with London a leading global financial centre. Key sectors include finance, professional services, education, creative industries, pharmaceuticals, and aerospace. Its largest trading partners are the EU and the United States.

## International Relations

Britain built its empire through conquest, slavery, and resource extraction, causing widespread suffering and lasting divisions in places like Ireland, India, and Kenya. After World War II most colonies became independent. Today it is a nuclear power and UN Security Council member, with a "special relationship" with the US.
