## History

According to tradition, San Marino was founded in 301 by a Christian stonemason, making it the world's oldest surviving republic. It stayed independent while Italy was unified, and Napoleon and Abraham Lincoln both admired it.

## People

San Marino is entirely surrounded by Italy. Most people speak Italian and are Catholic. Its two heads of state, the Captains Regent, change every six months.

## Economy

San Marino has a high income per person, based on tourism, banking, manufacturing, and the sale of stamps and coins. It uses the euro.

## International Relations

San Marino is not in the EU but has a customs union with it. Its closest partner is Italy, which it relies on for defence and many services.
