## History

Malacca was a powerful trading sultanate around 1400. It fell to the Portuguese, then the Dutch, and Britain gradually took control of the region. Malaya became independent in 1957 and formed Malaysia in 1963 with Sabah, Sarawak, and Singapore, which left in 1965.

## People

About two-thirds of people are Malay or Indigenous, with large Chinese and Indian minorities. Islam is the official religion, and Buddhism, Christianity, and Hinduism are also common. Malay is the national language.

## Economy

Malaysia has an upper-middle-income economy, based on electronics, semiconductors, palm oil, oil and gas, and tourism.

## International Relations

Malaysia is a founding member of ASEAN and a member of the Commonwealth. It disputes China's claims in the South China Sea, but has strong trade ties with China.
