## History

Ancient Greece gave rise to democracy, philosophy, and the Olympic Games. It was later part of the Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman empires, and won independence in 1830. It was occupied in World War II and suffered a civil war, then a military dictatorship from 1967 to 1974.

## People

Most people are Greek and Orthodox Christians. Athens and Thessaloniki are the largest cities. The population is ageing and shrinking, and many young Greeks emigrated during the debt crisis.

## Economy

Greece's economy is based on tourism, shipping, and agriculture. It suffered a severe debt crisis after 2009, needing several bailouts, and lost about a quarter of its income, but has since recovered.

## International Relations

Greece joined NATO in 1952 and the EU in 1981. It has a long rivalry with Turkey over airspace, islands, and Cyprus. Thousands of migrants cross from Turkey to its islands.
