## History

Nauru was settled by Micronesians and Polynesians. Germany annexed it in 1888, and after World War I it was run by Australia, New Zealand, and Britain. It became independent in 1968.

## People

Nauru is the world's smallest island country and third-smallest by area. Most people are Nauruan and Christian, and obesity rates are very high.

## Economy

Phosphate mining made Nauru briefly one of the richest countries per person, but the deposits are largely exhausted and much of the island is barren. It now relies on hosting an Australian immigration detention centre.

## International Relations

Nauru has close ties with Australia, which provides security and aid. It switched recognition from Taiwan to China in 2024.
