## History

Vietnam was ruled by China for one thousand years until 938. France colonized it in the 1800s. Vietnam declared independence in 1945 and fought France until 1954, when it was divided. In the Vietnam War, the US backed the South, but the communist North won in 1975 and reunited the country.

## People

Most people are Kinh (ethnic Vietnamese), with dozens of minorities. Most follow folk religion or Buddhism, or are non-religious. The population is young and growing.

## Economy

Vietnam has one of Asia's fastest-growing economies, a major manufacturer of electronics, clothing, and shoes. It is also a leading exporter of coffee and rice.

## International Relations

Vietnam has a communist government and is a member of ASEAN. It has close ties with both China and the US, and disputes China's claims in the South China Sea.
