## History

The Dutch settled the Cape in 1652, and Britain later took control. After 1948, the white minority government imposed apartheid, a system of racial segregation. Nelson Mandela, imprisoned for 27 years, became the first Black president in 1994 after free elections.

## People

South Africa has 12 official languages and a diverse population, mostly Black African, with white, mixed-race, and Indian minorities. Most people are Christian.

## Economy

South Africa has one of Africa's largest economies, relying on mining, especially gold and platinum, manufacturing, and services. Unemployment and inequality are among the highest in the world.

## International Relations

South Africa is a member of the G20, BRICS, the Commonwealth, and the African Union. It brought a genocide case against Israel to the International Court of Justice in 2023.
