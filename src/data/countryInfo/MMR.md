## History

Myanmar was home to Buddhist kingdoms, including Bagan, famous for its temples. Britain conquered it in the 1800s, and it became independent in 1948. The military ruled from 1962 to 2011. After a democratic opening, the army seized power again in 2021, starting a civil war.

## People

Most people are Bamar and Buddhist, with many minorities, such as the Shan, Karen, and Kachin. Most Rohingya Muslims fled to Bangladesh after a military crackdown in 2017.

## Economy

Myanmar is one of Asia's poorest countries. It relies on agriculture, natural gas, jade, and rare earths. The war has badly damaged the economy.

## International Relations

Myanmar's military government is under Western sanctions and relies on China and Russia. ASEAN has excluded its leaders from top meetings.
