## History

Mesopotamia, between the Tigris and Euphrates, was home to some of the earliest civilizations, including Sumer and Babylon. Baghdad was a great centre of the Islamic world. After Ottoman and British rule, Iraq became independent in 1932. Saddam Hussein ruled from 1979 until a US-led invasion in 2003, followed by years of conflict.

## People

Most Iraqis are Arabs, with a large Kurdish minority in the north, which has its own regional government. Most people are Muslim, split between a Shia majority and a Sunni minority. Arabic and Kurdish are official.

## Economy

Iraq's economy depends almost entirely on oil, which provides most government revenue. Years of war, corruption, and power cuts have held back development.

## International Relations

Iraq balances ties with the US and neighbouring Iran, which has strong influence through allied militias. The Islamic State group seized large areas in 2014 before being defeated in 2017.
