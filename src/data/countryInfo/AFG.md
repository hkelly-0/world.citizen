## History

Afghanistan has been a crossroads of empires. Modern Afghanistan emerged in the 1700s and resisted British invasions in the 1800s. The Soviet Union invaded in 1979, and a long war followed. The Taliban ruled from 1996 until a US-led invasion in 2001, and retook power in 2021.

## People

Afghanistan is diverse, with Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazaras, and Uzbeks. Pashto and Dari are the main languages, and almost all people are Muslim. Under the Taliban, girls are banned from secondary school and women face severe restrictions.

## Economy

Afghanistan is one of the world's poorest countries. Most people rely on farming, and the economy collapsed after foreign aid was cut in 2021. It has large untapped mineral deposits.

## International Relations

Only Russia formally recognizes the Taliban government. Afghanistan has tense relations with Pakistan, which has expelled many Afghan refugees.
