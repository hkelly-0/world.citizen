## History

Kenya's coast was a centre of Swahili trade for centuries. Britain colonized it in the 1890s, and white settlers took much of the best farmland. The Mau Mau uprising (1952–60) was brutally suppressed. Kenya became independent in 1963 under Jomo Kenyatta. Violence after the 2007 election killed over 1,000 people.

## People

Kenya has many ethnic groups, including the Kikuyu, Luhya, Kalenjin, and Luo. Most people are Christian, with a Muslim minority on the coast. English and Swahili are official. Its runners are famous worldwide.

## Economy

Kenya has East Africa's largest economy, relying on agriculture, especially tea and flowers, tourism, and technology. Its M-Pesa mobile money system is a global model. High debt has led to protests.

## International Relations

Kenya is a member of the Commonwealth and the East African Community, and Nairobi hosts UN headquarters for the environment. It led a security mission to Haiti from 2024.
