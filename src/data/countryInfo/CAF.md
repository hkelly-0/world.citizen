## History

The region was raided for slaves for centuries before France colonized it. It became independent in 1960. Jean-Bédel Bokassa seized power in 1966 and crowned himself emperor in 1976 before France helped remove him. A civil war began in 2012 between mainly Muslim Séléka rebels and Christian militias.

## People

The Central African Republic has many ethnic groups, including the Gbaya and Banda. Most people are Christian, with a Muslim minority. French and Sango are official. Many people have been displaced by fighting.

## Economy

The Central African Republic is one of the world's poorest countries, despite diamonds, gold, timber, and uranium. Armed groups profit from mining, and most people rely on farming.

## International Relations

The Central African Republic relies on UN peacekeepers and Russian mercenaries for security, and its ties with France have weakened.
