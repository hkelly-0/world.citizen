## History

Samoa was settled about 3,000 years ago. Germany ruled it from 1900, then New Zealand from 1914. A flu epidemic in 1918 killed about a fifth of the population. Samoans resisted with the non-violent Mau movement. Samoa became the first Pacific island nation to gain independence, in 1962.

## People

Almost all people are Samoan and Christian. Traditional customs, called fa'a Samoa, and village chiefs remain central to life. Many Samoans live in New Zealand, Australia, and the US.

## Economy

Samoa's economy relies on money sent home by Samoans abroad, tourism, agriculture, and fishing. In 2011 it moved across the International Date Line to align with Australia and New Zealand.

## International Relations

Samoa has close ties with New Zealand and is a member of the Commonwealth and the Pacific Islands Forum.
