## History

After Japanese rule ended in 1945, the Soviet Union backed a communist state in the north under Kim Il Sung. He invaded the South in 1950, starting the Korean War. The Kim family has ruled ever since, now under Kim Jong Un. A famine in the 1990s killed hundreds of thousands.

## People

Almost all people are ethnic Koreans. The state controls nearly all aspects of life, and the country is one of the most isolated in the world, with severe human rights abuses.

## Economy

North Korea has a state-controlled economy that is very poor and depends heavily on trade with China. It is under heavy UN sanctions.

## International Relations

North Korea has developed nuclear weapons and missiles, defying UN resolutions. It is closely aligned with China and Russia, and has sent troops to help Russia fight Ukraine.
