## History

Somalia's coast was a trading hub for centuries. Britain and Italy colonized it, and the two parts united at independence in 1960. Siad Barre ruled as a dictator until 1991, when the state collapsed into civil war and famine. Somaliland in the north declared independence, though it is not widely recognized.

## People

Almost all people are Somali and Sunni Muslim, and society is organized by clans. Somali and Arabic are official. Many Somalis live abroad, especially in Kenya, the Gulf, Europe, and North America.

## Economy

Somalia's economy relies on livestock, money sent home by Somalis abroad, and telecommunications. Droughts often cause famine.

## International Relations

Somalia is fighting the Islamist group al-Shabaab, with African Union troops. Piracy off its coast peaked around 2011. It has tension with Ethiopia over Somaliland.
