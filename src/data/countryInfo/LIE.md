## History

Liechtenstein was created in 1719 as a principality of the Holy Roman Empire, named after its ruling family. It became sovereign in 1806 and has been neutral since 1868, abolishing its army. Its princes still have significant political power.

## People

Liechtenstein is one of the world's smallest countries. Most people speak German and are Catholic. About a third of residents are foreign citizens, and many workers commute from neighbouring countries.

## Economy

Liechtenstein has one of the highest incomes per person in the world, based on finance and specialized manufacturing, such as dental products and tools. It uses the Swiss franc.

## International Relations

Liechtenstein is in a customs union with Switzerland, which also represents it abroad in many places. It is not in the EU but takes part in its single market.
