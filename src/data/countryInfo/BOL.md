## History

Bolivia was part of the Inca Empire and, under Spain, the source of enormous silver wealth from Potosí. It gained independence in 1825 and was named after Simón Bolívar. It lost its coastline to Chile in 1884 and land to Brazil and Paraguay. In 2006, Evo Morales became its first Indigenous president.

## People

Bolivia has the largest Indigenous share of any country in South America, mainly Quechua and Aymara. It has 37 official languages. La Paz is the seat of government, and Sucre is the constitutional capital.

## Economy

Bolivia is one of South America's poorer countries. It relies on natural gas, mining (zinc, silver, tin, and gold), and soybeans. It has some of the world's largest lithium reserves, which are largely untapped.

## International Relations

Bolivia is landlocked and still seeks access to the Pacific, although the International Court of Justice ruled against its claim in 2018. It is a member of Mercosur and has close ties with its neighbours.
