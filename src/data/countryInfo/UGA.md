## History

Uganda was home to the Kingdom of Buganda and other kingdoms. Britain made it a protectorate in 1894, and it became independent in 1962. Idi Amin seized power in 1971 and ruled brutally, killing up to 300,000 people and expelling the Asian community. Yoweri Museveni took power in 1986 and has ruled since.

## People

Uganda has many ethnic groups, the largest being the Baganda, and one of the world's youngest populations. Most people are Christian, with a Muslim minority. English and Swahili are official.

## Economy

Uganda's economy relies on agriculture, especially coffee, as well as gold and services. Oil fields near Lake Albert are being developed, with an export pipeline through Tanzania.

## International Relations

Uganda is a member of the Commonwealth and the East African Community. It hosts more refugees than any other African country, and its troops have served in Somalia and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
