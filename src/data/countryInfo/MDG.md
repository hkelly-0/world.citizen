## History

Madagascar was settled by people from Southeast Asia, probably Borneo, and later East Africa. The Merina Kingdom united much of the island in the 1800s before France colonized it in 1896. A 1947 uprising was brutally crushed. It became independent in 1960. Political crises have been frequent, and in 2025 protests led to a military takeover.

## People

Most people are Malagasy, of mixed Southeast Asian and African descent. Christianity and traditional religions, including ancestor veneration, are both common. Malagasy and French are official.

## Economy

Madagascar is one of the world's poorest countries. It is the world's largest producer of vanilla, and also exports nickel, cobalt, and clothing. Most of its wildlife, including lemurs, is found nowhere else on Earth.

## International Relations

Madagascar is a member of the African Union and the Southern African Development Community, and has close ties with France.
