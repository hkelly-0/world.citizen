## History

Luxembourg was ruled by various European powers and became an independent grand duchy in 1867, declared permanently neutral. Germany occupied it in both World Wars. It is the world's only remaining grand duchy.

## People

Nearly half of residents are foreign citizens, especially from Portugal, France, Italy, and Belgium, and many more workers cross the border every day. Luxembourgish, French, and German are all official.

## Economy

Luxembourg has one of the highest incomes per person in the world. Once based on steel, its economy is now led by banking, investment funds, and EU institutions. Public transport is free.

## International Relations

Luxembourg is a founding member of the EU and NATO, and hosts the EU's Court of Justice. It works closely with Belgium and the Netherlands in the Benelux union.
