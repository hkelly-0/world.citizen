## History

Sri Lanka had ancient Buddhist kingdoms, then was colonized by the Portuguese, Dutch, and British. It became independent in 1948. A civil war (1983–2009) between the government and the Tamil Tigers killed tens of thousands. Economic collapse in 2022 led to mass protests that forced out the president.

## People

Most people are Sinhalese and Buddhist, with a large Tamil minority, mostly Hindu, as well as Muslims and Christians. Sinhala and Tamil are both official.

## Economy

Sri Lanka's economy is based on tea, clothing, tourism, and money sent home by migrant workers. It defaulted on its debts in 2022 and has since been recovering with IMF support.

## International Relations

Sri Lanka balances ties with India and China, which has funded major ports and infrastructure. It is a member of the Commonwealth.
