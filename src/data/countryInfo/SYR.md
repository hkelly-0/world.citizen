## History

Damascus is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities. After Ottoman and French rule, Syria became independent in 1946. The Assad family ruled from 1970. Protests in 2011 led to a civil war that killed hundreds of thousands, until rebels overthrew Bashar al-Assad in 2024.

## People

Most Syrians are Arabs and Sunni Muslims, with Kurdish, Alawite, Christian, and Druze minorities. Over half the population was displaced by the war, and millions remain refugees abroad.

## Economy

Syria's economy has been devastated by war. It once relied on oil, agriculture, and textiles. Reconstruction has begun as some sanctions have been lifted.

## International Relations

Syria's new government is rebuilding ties with Arab states, Turkey, and the West, after the Assad government relied on Russia and Iran. Israel occupies the Golan Heights.
