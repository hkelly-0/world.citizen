## History

Great Zimbabwe was the stone capital of a medieval kingdom. Britain colonized the region as Southern Rhodesia, and a white minority government declared independence in 1965. After a war, Zimbabwe became independent in 1980 under Robert Mugabe. Thousands were killed in the Gukurahundi massacres in the 1980s, and Mugabe ruled until a 2017 coup.

## People

Most people are Shona, with a Ndebele minority. Most are Christian. Zimbabwe has 16 official languages. Millions of Zimbabweans have emigrated, especially to South Africa.

## Economy

Zimbabwe's economy relies on mining, especially gold, platinum, and lithium, as well as tobacco and tourism. Land seizures from white farmers in the 2000s led to economic collapse and hyperinflation.

## International Relations

Zimbabwe left the Commonwealth in 2003 and faces Western sanctions. It is a member of the Southern African Development Community and has close ties with China.
