## History

Brunei was once a powerful sultanate ruling much of Borneo and parts of the Philippines. It lost most of its territory in the 1800s and became a British protectorate in 1888. Oil was found in 1929, and it became independent in 1984.

## People

Most people are Malay and Muslim, with a Chinese minority. Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah, one of the world's longest-reigning monarchs, rules with absolute power. Brunei has introduced sharia law.

## Economy

Brunei has a high income per person from oil and gas, which make up most of its exports. Citizens pay no income tax and receive free healthcare and education.

## International Relations

Brunei is a member of ASEAN and the Commonwealth. It claims part of the South China Sea.
