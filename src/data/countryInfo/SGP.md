## History

Britain founded Singapore as a trading post in 1819. Japan occupied it in World War II. It joined Malaysia in 1963 but was expelled in 1965, becoming independent. Under Lee Kuan Yew, it grew from a poor port into one of the world's richest countries.

## People

Most people are of Chinese descent, with Malay and Indian minorities. English, Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil are all official. It is one of the most densely populated countries in the world.

## Economy

Singapore has one of the world's highest incomes per person. It is a global hub for finance, shipping, trade, and electronics manufacturing, with one of the world's busiest ports.

## International Relations

Singapore is a founding member of ASEAN and a member of the Commonwealth. It keeps close ties with both the US and China.
