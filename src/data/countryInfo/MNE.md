## History

Montenegro's mountains helped it resist Ottoman rule, and it was recognized as independent in 1878. It joined Yugoslavia after World War I, then stayed in a union with Serbia after Yugoslavia broke up. It became independent after a referendum in 2006.

## People

Montenegro has a small population, roughly split between people who call themselves Montenegrin and Serb, with Bosniak, Albanian, and other minorities. Most are Orthodox Christians.

## Economy

Montenegro's economy relies on tourism along its Adriatic coast, as well as energy and services. It uses the euro, though it is not in the eurozone.

## International Relations

Montenegro joined NATO in 2017 and is a leading candidate to join the EU. Its politics remain divided between pro-Western and pro-Serbian parties.
