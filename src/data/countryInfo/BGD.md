## History

Bengal was part of the Mughal Empire and later British India. In 1947 it became East Pakistan. After a brutal war in 1971, in which hundreds of thousands were killed, it became independent with India's help. In 2024, student protests overthrew Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina.

## People

Bangladesh is one of the world's most densely populated countries. Almost everyone speaks Bengali, and most are Muslim, with a Hindu minority. It hosts around one million Rohingya refugees from Myanmar.

## Economy

Bangladesh's economy has grown quickly, driven by the clothing industry, the world's second-largest after China's. Money sent home by migrant workers is also important. Poverty has fallen sharply.

## International Relations

Bangladesh has close ties with India, though relations have cooled since 2024. China is a major investor. It is very vulnerable to climate change and rising seas.
