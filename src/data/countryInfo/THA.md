## History

Thailand, formerly Siam, grew from the kingdoms of Sukhothai and Ayutthaya. It is the only Southeast Asian country never colonized by Europeans. Absolute monarchy ended in 1932, and the military has seized power many times since.

## People

Most people are Thai and Buddhist, with a Malay Muslim minority in the south. Thai is the official language. The king is deeply respected, and criticism of the monarchy is illegal.

## Economy

Thailand has a large, upper-middle-income economy, led by car and electronics manufacturing, tourism, and agriculture, especially rice and rubber.

## International Relations

Thailand is a founding member of ASEAN and a long-standing US ally, while also close to China. It had a border conflict with Cambodia in 2025.
