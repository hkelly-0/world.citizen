## History

The Magyars settled Hungary in 896, and it became a kingdom in 1000. After Ottoman rule, it became part of the Habsburg Empire. After World War I, it lost two-thirds of its territory. Under Soviet-backed communist rule from 1949, a 1956 uprising was crushed. Communism ended in 1989.

## People

Most people are ethnic Hungarians and speak Hungarian, a language unrelated to most European ones. Large Hungarian communities live in neighbouring countries. The population is shrinking.

## Economy

Hungary has a high-income economy, with car manufacturing by German companies, electronics, and pharmaceuticals. A growing number of Chinese factories, including battery plants, are also based there.

## International Relations

Hungary joined NATO in 1999 and the EU in 2004. Under Viktor Orbán, it clashed with the EU over democracy and the rule of law, and kept friendlier ties with Russia and China than its allies.
