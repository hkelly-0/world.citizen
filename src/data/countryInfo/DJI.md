## History

The region was home to Afar and Somali peoples and was part of trade routes across the Red Sea. France colonized it in the 1800s as French Somaliland. It became independent in 1977. A civil war with Afar rebels ran in the 1990s. Ismaïl Omar Guelleh has been president since 1999.

## People

Most people are Somali or Afar and Sunni Muslim. French and Arabic are official. Most people live in the capital, Djibouti City, as much of the country is desert.

## Economy

Djibouti's economy relies on its port, which handles most of landlocked Ethiopia's trade, linked by a railway built by China. Fees from foreign military bases are also important.

## International Relations

Djibouti lies at the entrance to the Red Sea, one of the world's busiest shipping routes, and hosts military bases for the US, France, China, Japan, and Italy.
