## History

The region was home to Afar and Somali peoples and was part of trade routes across the Red Sea. [France]{FRA} colonized it in the 1800s as French Somaliland. It became independent in 1977. A civil war with Afar rebels ran in the 1990s. Ismaïl Omar Guelleh has been president since 1999.

## People

Most people are Somali or Afar and Sunni Muslim. French and Arabic are official. Most people live in the capital, Djibouti City, as much of the country is desert.

## Economy

Djibouti's economy relies on its port, which handles most of landlocked [Ethiopia]{ETH}'s trade, linked by a railway built by [China]{CHN}. Fees from foreign military bases are also important.

## International Relations

Djibouti lies at the entrance to the Red Sea, one of the world's busiest shipping routes, and hosts military bases for the [US]{USA}, [France]{FRA}, [China]{CHN}, [Japan]{JPN}, and [Italy]{ITA}.
