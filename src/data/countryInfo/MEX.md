## History

Mexico was home to great civilizations, including the Olmec, Maya, and Aztec. Spain conquered the Aztec Empire in 1521 and ruled for three centuries. Mexico won independence in 1821, then lost half its territory to the United States in 1848. A revolution from 1910 reshaped the country, which was ruled by one party until 2000.

## People

Most Mexicans are mestizo, of mixed Indigenous and European descent, and millions speak Indigenous languages such as Nahuatl and Maya. Spanish is dominant, and most people are Catholic. Mexico City is one of the world's largest cities.

## Economy

Mexico has a large, upper-middle-income economy built on manufacturing, especially cars and electronics, along with oil, mining, agriculture, and tourism. Most exports go to the United States. Inequality is high, and drug cartels are a major problem.

## International Relations

Mexico's foreign policy has long stressed non-intervention. It is bound closely to the United States through trade and migration, sharing a free-trade agreement with it and Canada. It belongs to the G20 and is an influential voice in Latin America.
