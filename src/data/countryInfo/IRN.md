## History

Iran, long known as Persia, was home to great empires, beginning with the Achaemenids in the 500s BC. It became Muslim after Arab conquest in the 600s and Shia under the Safavids around 1500. In 1953 a UK and US-backed coup restored the Shah, who was overthrown in the 1979 Islamic Revolution.

## People

Most people speak Persian, with large Azerbaijani, Kurdish, and other minorities. Almost all are Muslim, mostly Shia. The population is young and highly educated, and many women have protested strict dress rules.

## Economy

Iran has some of the world's largest oil and gas reserves. Its economy also includes manufacturing and agriculture, but has been weakened by international sanctions, high inflation, and state control.

## International Relations

Iran is a regional power, backing groups such as Hezbollah and the Houthis. Its nuclear programme has led to sanctions and conflict with the US and Israel, which bombed its nuclear sites in 2025.
