## History

Slovakia was part of Hungary for centuries, then joined Czechoslovakia in 1918. During World War II it was a Nazi puppet state. After the war it was under communist rule until the Velvet Revolution of 1989. It became independent in 1993 after a peaceful split from the Czech Republic.

## People

Most people are ethnic Slovaks and speak Slovak. There is a large Hungarian minority in the south and a Roma community. Most people are Catholic.

## Economy

Slovakia makes more cars per person than any other country in the world, with factories from Volkswagen, Kia, and others. Its economy relies heavily on manufacturing and exports.

## International Relations

Slovakia joined NATO and the EU in 2004 and uses the euro. Its government has recently taken a softer line on Russia than most of its EU neighbours.
