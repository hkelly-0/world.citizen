## History

The Tswana chiefs asked Britain for protection against Boer expansion, and Bechuanaland became a British protectorate in 1885. At independence in 1966, it was one of the world's poorest countries. Under Seretse Khama, its first president, it became a stable democracy. In 2024 the ruling party lost power for the first time.

## People

Most people are Tswana and Christian. English is official, and Setswana is widely spoken. The Kalahari Desert covers most of the country, and the San are among its oldest inhabitants.

## Economy

Diamonds transformed Botswana into one of Africa's richest countries per person, and they are managed jointly with De Beers. Tourism, especially safaris in the Okavango Delta, is also important. HIV hit the country hard but is now well controlled.

## International Relations

Botswana is a member of the Commonwealth and the Southern African Development Community, which has its headquarters in Gaborone. It is known for low corruption and good governance.
