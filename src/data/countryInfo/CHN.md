## History

China is one of the world's oldest civilizations, ruled by dynasties for over two thousand years. In the 1800s and 1900s it suffered foreign invasions and civil war. In 1949 Mao Zedong's Communists founded the People's Republic. Reforms from 1978 under Deng Xiaoping turned it into an economic power.

## People

China is the world's second-most populous country. Most people are Han Chinese, with 55 recognized minorities, including Uyghurs and Tibetans. Mandarin is the national language, and many other Chinese languages are spoken. The population is ageing.

## Economy

China has the world's second-largest economy and is the biggest manufacturer and exporter. It leads in electric cars, solar panels, and batteries. Rapid growth lifted hundreds of millions out of poverty.

## International Relations

China is a nuclear power and permanent UN Security Council member, a rival of the US. Its Belt and Road Initiative funds infrastructure worldwide. It claims Taiwan and most of the South China Sea.
