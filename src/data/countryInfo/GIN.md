## History

Guinea was part of the Mali and Songhai empires. France colonized it, and in 1958 it was the first French colony in Africa to vote for independence. Ahmed Sékou Touré ruled harshly until 1984, followed by Lansana Conté until 2008. An Ebola epidemic struck in 2014–16, and the military took power in 2021.

## People

Guinea has many ethnic groups, including the Fula, Malinke, and Susu. Most people are Muslim. French is official. The Niger, Senegal, and Gambia rivers all begin in its highlands.

## Economy

Guinea has the world's largest reserves of bauxite, used to make aluminium, and huge iron ore deposits at Simandou, developed with Chinese investment. Despite this, most people are poor.

## International Relations

Guinea is a member of ECOWAS. Its military government has promised a return to civilian rule.
