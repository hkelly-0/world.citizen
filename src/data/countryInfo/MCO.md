## History

The Grimaldi family has ruled Monaco since 1297. It became a French protectorate and lost most of its land in 1861, but kept its independence. The casino at Monte Carlo, opened in the 1860s, made the country rich. Prince Albert II has reigned since 2005.

## People

Monaco is the world's second-smallest country and the most densely populated. Most residents are foreign citizens, mainly French and Italian; native Monegasques are a minority. French is the official language.

## Economy

Monaco's economy is built on tourism, gambling, finance, and property. Its residents pay no income tax, making it a home for the very wealthy. The Monaco Grand Prix is a major event.

## International Relations

Monaco's defence is the responsibility of France, with which it has close ties. It is not in the EU but uses the euro and is a member of the UN.
