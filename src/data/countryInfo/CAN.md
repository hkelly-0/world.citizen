## History

North America is home to hundreds of Indigenous nations, whose populations and cultures were greatly diminished by British and French colonization, beginning around 1600. Over the following centuries, the provinces of Canada were gradually united, beginning with Confederation in 1867. Canada remains part of the British Commonwealth, although it now has full independence.

## People

Most Canadians live near the southern border where winters are milder. European ancestry is common, with growing South Asian and East asian communities due to high immigration. English and French are official. Most people are Christian or non-religious.

## Economy

Canada has an advanced economy, built on abundant natural resources (energy, minerals, forestry, agriculture), with strong services, manufacturing, and finance sectors. Trade is vital, especially with the United States.

## International Relations

Canada fought alongside Britain in both World Wars, then helped found the UN and NATO and pioneered UN peacekeeping. Today it favours multilateralism, belongs to the G7, G20, and Commonwealth, and is closest to its largest trading partner, the United States.
