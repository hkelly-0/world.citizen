## History

Muslims ruled much of Spain from 711 until 1492, when Spain also began building a huge empire in the Americas. Its power declined in the 1800s. Spain's civil war (1936–39) led to Francisco Franco's dictatorship, which lasted until his death in 1975, when Spain became a democracy.

## People

Spanish is dominant, while Catalan, Basque, and Galician are co-official in their regions. Catalonia has a strong independence movement. Many residents are immigrants from Latin America, Morocco, and Romania.

## Economy

Spain has a large economy based on tourism, services, cars, and agriculture, especially olive oil, fruit, and wine. It is a world leader in renewable energy, but unemployment has long been high.

## International Relations

Spain joined NATO in 1982 and the EU in 1986, and has strong ties with Latin America. It claims Gibraltar from the UK, while its own cities of Ceuta and Melilla in North Africa are claimed by Morocco.
