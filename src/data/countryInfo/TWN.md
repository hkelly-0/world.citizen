## History

Taiwan was home to Austronesian peoples before Chinese settlers arrived from the 1600s. It was ruled by the Qing dynasty, then by Japan from 1895 to 1945. After losing China's civil war in 1949, the Republic of China government moved to Taiwan. Martial law ended in 1987, and Taiwan became a democracy in the 1990s.

## People

Most people are Han Chinese, and about 2% belong to Indigenous peoples. Mandarin is the main language, alongside Taiwanese Hokkien and Hakka. Taiwan has one of the world's lowest birth rates, and its population is ageing.

## Economy

Taiwan is a wealthy, export-driven economy. It makes most of the world's most advanced computer chips, led by TSMC, and is a major electronics producer.

## International Relations

Taiwan governs itself but is claimed by China, which has not ruled out force to take it. Only about a dozen countries recognize it diplomatically, and it is not a UN member. The US is its main security partner.
