## History

Tonga was the centre of a powerful maritime empire from around the 900s. It was never colonized, though it was a British protected state from 1900 to 1970. It has been a constitutional monarchy since 1875, and reforms in 2010 strengthened parliament.

## People

Almost all people are Tongan and Christian. More Tongans live abroad than in Tonga, mainly in New Zealand, Australia, and the US.

## Economy

Tonga's economy relies on money sent home by Tongans abroad, agriculture, fishing, and tourism. A volcanic eruption and tsunami in 2022 caused major damage.

## International Relations

Tonga is a member of the Commonwealth and the Pacific Islands Forum. China is a major lender.
