## History

Turkmenistan's oasis city of Merv was a great Silk Road centre. Russia conquered the region in the 1880s, and it became a Soviet republic. Since independence in 1991, it has been one of the world's most closed countries, ruled first by Saparmurat Niyazov, then by the Berdimuhamedow family.

## People

Most people are ethnic Turkmens and Sunni Muslims, with Uzbek and Russian minorities. Most of the country is covered by the Karakum Desert.

## Economy

Turkmenistan has some of the world's largest natural gas reserves, mostly exported to China. Its economy is state-controlled, and cotton is also important.

## International Relations

Turkmenistan has been officially neutral since 1995, recognized by the UN. It keeps out of most international alliances and limits contact with the outside world.
