## History

Kyrgyzstan's nomadic people lived in the Tian Shan mountains for centuries. Russia conquered the region in the 1800s, and it became a Soviet republic. Since independence in 1991, revolutions have overthrown three presidents, in 2005, 2010, and 2020. Ethnic violence between Kyrgyz and Uzbeks killed hundreds in 2010.

## People

Most people are ethnic Kyrgyz and Sunni Muslims, with Uzbek and Russian minorities. Kyrgyz and Russian are both official. Mountains cover most of the country, and nomadic traditions such as yurts remain important.

## Economy

Kyrgyzstan's economy relies on gold mining, agriculture, and money sent home by migrants, mainly from Russia. It re-exports many Chinese goods.

## International Relations

Kyrgyzstan is a close ally of Russia, which has a military base there, and is part of the Eurasian Economic Union. China is an important trading partner, and its government has become more authoritarian.
