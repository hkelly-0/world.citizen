## History

Paraguay was colonized by Spain, and Jesuit missions to the Indigenous Guaraní flourished there. It gained independence in 1811. In the Paraguayan War (1864–70) against Brazil, Argentina, and Uruguay, it lost most of its men. Alfredo Stroessner ruled as dictator from 1954 to 1989.

## People

Most Paraguayans are mestizo. It is the only country in the Americas where most people speak an Indigenous language, Guaraní, alongside Spanish. Most people are Catholic.

## Economy

Paraguay's economy relies on agriculture, especially soybeans and beef. The huge Itaipú Dam, shared with Brazil, produces almost all its electricity, and it exports the rest.

## International Relations

Paraguay is a founding member of Mercosur and is landlocked, relying on rivers to reach the sea. It is the only South American country that recognizes Taiwan instead of China.
