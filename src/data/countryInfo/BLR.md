## History

Belarus was part of Kievan Rus', then Poland-Lithuania, and from the late 1700s the Russian Empire. It was a Soviet republic, and lost about a quarter of its population in World War II. It became independent in 1991. Alexander Lukashenko has ruled since 1994, crushing large protests in 2020.

## People

Most people are ethnic Belarusians, with Russian and Polish minorities. Russian is more widely spoken than Belarusian, though both are official. Most are Orthodox Christians.

## Economy

Belarus has a largely state-run economy based on manufacturing, especially tractors and trucks, potash fertilizer, and refining Russian oil. It depends heavily on Russia for trade and energy.

## International Relations

Belarus is Russia's closest ally, part of a "Union State" with it, and let Russian forces use its territory to invade Ukraine in 2022. It is under heavy Western sanctions.
