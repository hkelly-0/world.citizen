## History

El Salvador was colonized by Spain in the 1520s and became independent in 1821. Coffee wealth was held by a small elite. A civil war (1980–92) between the US-backed government and left-wing guerrillas killed about 75,000 people. Later, gang violence soared, and a crackdown since 2022 has jailed tens of thousands.

## People

El Salvador is the smallest and most densely populated country in Central America. Most people are mestizo and speak Spanish. A large share of Salvadorans live abroad, mainly in the United States.

## Economy

El Salvador's economy depends on services, textiles, and money sent home by migrants, which make up about a quarter of its income. It uses the US dollar, and in 2021 became the first country to make Bitcoin legal tender.

## International Relations

El Salvador's closest partner is the United States, home to its largest diaspora. It belongs to the Central American Integration System. Its president's crackdown on gangs has been popular at home but criticized by human rights groups.
