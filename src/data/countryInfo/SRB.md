## History

Serbia was a medieval kingdom, then ruled by the Ottomans until gaining independence in the 1800s. It led Yugoslavia after World War I. In the 1990s, under Slobodan Milošević, it fought wars as Yugoslavia broke up, and NATO bombed it in 1999 over Kosovo. Montenegro separated in 2006.

## People

Most people are ethnic Serbs and Orthodox Christians, with Hungarian, Bosniak, and Roma minorities. Serbian is written in both Cyrillic and Latin scripts. The population is shrinking.

## Economy

Serbia's economy is based on services, cars, agriculture, and mining. Investment from China and the EU, its largest trading partner, has grown in recent years.

## International Relations

Serbia is an EU candidate but stays militarily neutral and keeps close ties with Russia and China. It does not recognize the independence of Kosovo, which declared independence in 2008.
