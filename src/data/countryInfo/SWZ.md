## History

The Swazi kingdom formed in the 1700s and became a British protectorate after the Boer War. It became independent in 1968. King Sobhuza II banned political parties in 1973. His son, King Mswati III, has ruled since 1986 as one of the world's last absolute monarchs, and the country was renamed Eswatini in 2018.

## People

Almost everyone is Swazi, and most people are Christian. Siswati and English are official. Traditional ceremonies remain important. It has one of the world's highest HIV rates.

## Economy

Eswatini's economy relies on sugar, soft drink concentrates, and textiles, and it depends heavily on trade with South Africa. Much of the population is poor, while the royal family is very wealthy.

## International Relations

Eswatini is a member of the Commonwealth and the Southern African Development Community. It is the only African country that recognizes Taiwan instead of China. Pro-democracy protests in 2021 were violently suppressed.
