## History

Bhutan was united in the 1600s and kept its independence through history. The Wangchuck dynasty has ruled since 1907. The king introduced democracy in 2008, creating a constitutional monarchy.

## People

Most people are Buddhist, with a Hindu minority of Nepali origin, many of whom were expelled in the 1990s. Dzongkha is the national language. Bhutan measures success by "Gross National Happiness".

## Economy

Bhutan's economy relies on hydropower sold to India, agriculture, and tourism, which it limits with a daily fee. Forests cover most of the country, and it absorbs more carbon than it emits.

## International Relations

Bhutan has very close ties with India, its main partner. It has no formal diplomatic relations with China, with which it has a border dispute.
