## History

Spain colonized Jamaica in 1494, wiping out the Indigenous Taíno, and Britain seized it in 1655. It became one of Britain's richest sugar colonies, worked by enslaved Africans until slavery was abolished in 1834. Jamaica gained independence in 1962.

## People

Most Jamaicans are of African descent. English is official, but Jamaican Patois is spoken by almost everyone. Jamaica has a large diaspora in the UK, US, and Canada, and is famous for reggae music and sprinters.

## Economy

Jamaica's economy relies on tourism, bauxite and aluminium, and money sent home by Jamaicans abroad. It has cut its once very high public debt, but crime and unemployment remain serious problems.

## International Relations

Jamaica is a member of the Commonwealth and the Caribbean Community (CARICOM), whose secretariat it helped set up. The British monarch remains head of state, though there is debate about becoming a republic. Its closest partners are the US and UK.
