## History

Albania was ruled by the Ottomans for over four centuries and became independent in 1912. Under Enver Hoxha, it was one of the world's most isolated communist dictatorships, from 1944 until 1985. Communism ended in 1991.

## People

Most people are ethnic Albanians and speak Albanian. Muslims are the largest religious group, with Orthodox and Catholic minorities. Many Albanians live abroad, especially in Italy and Greece.

## Economy

Albania's economy has grown since the 1990s and is based on services, tourism, agriculture, and energy, mostly from hydropower. Money sent home by Albanians abroad is important.

## International Relations

Albania joined NATO in 2009 and is a candidate to join the EU. It has close ties with Kosovo, where most people are ethnic Albanians, and with Italy.
