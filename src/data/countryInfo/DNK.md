## History

Denmark was a Viking power and in the Middle Ages ruled a union with Norway and Sweden. It lost Norway in 1814 and southern Jutland to Prussia in 1864, regaining part of it in 1920. Germany occupied Denmark during World War II. Greenland and the Faroe Islands remain part of the Danish kingdom.

## People

Most people are ethnic Danes and speak Danish, with growing immigrant communities. Most belong to the Lutheran state church, though few attend regularly. Danes regularly rank among the happiest people in the world.

## Economy

Denmark has a wealthy economy with a large welfare state. Key sectors include pharmaceuticals, shipping, wind energy, and food. Companies such as Novo Nordisk, Maersk, and Lego are world-famous.

## International Relations

Denmark is a founding member of NATO and joined the EU in 1973, though it kept the krone. It strongly supports Ukraine. US interest in acquiring Greenland has strained relations with Washington.
