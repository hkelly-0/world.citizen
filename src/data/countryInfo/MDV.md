## History

The Maldives converted to Islam in the 1100s and was ruled by sultans for centuries. It was a British protectorate until independence in 1965 and became a republic in 1968. Its first democratic elections were held in 2008.

## People

The Maldives is Asia's smallest country, made up of about 1,200 coral islands. Almost all citizens are Sunni Muslim, as required by law, and speak Dhivehi. About a third of people live in Malé.

## Economy

Tourism, especially luxury island resorts, dominates the economy, along with fishing. Public debt is high.

## International Relations

The Maldives is the world's lowest-lying country, and rising seas threaten its existence, making it a leading voice on climate change. It balances ties between India and China.
