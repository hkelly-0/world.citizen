## History

Bosnia was ruled by the Ottomans for four centuries, then by Austria-Hungary. It was part of Yugoslavia from 1918. After it declared independence in 1992, a war killed about 100,000 people, including the Srebrenica genocide. The 1995 Dayton Agreement split it into two entities with a weak central government.

## People

The three main groups are Bosniaks (mostly Muslim), Serbs (mostly Orthodox), and Croats (mostly Catholic). They speak Bosnian, Serbian, and Croatian, which are very similar. Many people emigrated during and after the war.

## Economy

Bosnia's economy is based on services, metals, energy, and wood. Unemployment is high, and a complicated government makes reforms difficult.

## International Relations

Bosnia is an EU candidate. An international High Representative oversees the peace agreement, and Serb leaders have threatened secession. Relations with neighbours Serbia and Croatia remain sensitive.
