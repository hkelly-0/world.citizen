## History

Anatolia was home to ancient civilizations and later the heart of the Byzantine Empire. The Ottoman Empire, based in Istanbul from 1453, ruled much of southeastern Europe and the Middle East. After its collapse in World War I, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk founded the secular Republic of Turkey in 1923.

## People

Most people are ethnic Turks and Muslim, with a large Kurdish minority, mainly in the southeast. Turkish is the official language. Turkey hosts millions of refugees, mostly from Syria.

## Economy

Turkey has a large, diverse economy, with strong manufacturing, especially cars, textiles, and steel, as well as agriculture, construction, and tourism. It has suffered high inflation in recent years.

## International Relations

Turkey joined NATO in 1952 and controls the straits linking the Black Sea to the Mediterranean. It is an EU candidate, but talks have stalled. It plays a major role in Syria, and has long fought Kurdish militants.
