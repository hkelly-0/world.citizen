## History

Mozambique's coast was part of Swahili trade networks. Portugal colonized it from the 1500s. FRELIMO fought a war of independence, which came in 1975. A civil war (1977–92) between FRELIMO and RENAMO, backed by white-ruled Rhodesia and South Africa, killed about one million people. An Islamist insurgency began in the north in 2017.

## People

Mozambique has many ethnic groups. Most people are Christian, with a large Muslim minority in the north. Portuguese is official, but many people speak only local languages.

## Economy

Mozambique is one of the world's poorest countries, relying on agriculture, coal, aluminium, and large offshore natural gas fields. It is often hit by cyclones.

## International Relations

Mozambique joined the Commonwealth in 1995, despite having no British colonial history, and is a member of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries. Rwandan troops help fight the insurgency.
