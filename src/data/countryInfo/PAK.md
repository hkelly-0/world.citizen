## History

The Indus Valley Civilization flourished here thousands of years ago. The region later came under the Mughal Empire and then British rule. Pakistan was created in 1947 as a homeland for Muslims during the Partition of India. East Pakistan broke away as Bangladesh in 1971. The military has ruled for long periods.

## People

Pakistan is the world's fifth-most populous country. Most people are Muslim. Punjabis are the largest group, along with Pashtuns, Sindhis, and Baloch. Urdu and English are official.

## Economy

Pakistan's economy relies on agriculture, textiles, and services, and money sent home by workers abroad. It has often needed loans from the IMF.

## International Relations

Pakistan is a nuclear power and has fought several wars with India, mostly over Kashmir. China is its closest ally. Relations with Afghanistan are tense.
