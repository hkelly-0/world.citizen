## History

The Solomon Islands were settled thousands of years ago and became a British protectorate in 1893. They saw fierce fighting in World War II, especially on Guadalcanal. Independence came in 1978. Ethnic violence from 1998 to 2003 led to an Australian-led peacekeeping mission.

## People

Most people are Melanesian and Christian, living in rural villages. Over 70 languages are spoken, with Solomon Islands Pijin widely used.

## Economy

The Solomon Islands is one of the Pacific's poorest countries, relying on logging, fishing, and agriculture.

## International Relations

The Solomon Islands switched recognition from Taiwan to China in 2019, and signed a security pact with China in 2022, worrying Australia and the US.
