## History

The English settled Antigua in 1632 and established sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, while Barbuda was used to supply food. Slavery was abolished in 1834. The islands gained independence together in 1981.

## People

Most people are of African descent and speak English and Antiguan Creole. Nearly everyone lives on Antigua; Barbuda has under 2,000 residents and was badly damaged by Hurricane Irma in 2017.

## Economy

Tourism dominates the economy, helped by its many beaches and sailing events. The country also sells citizenship to foreign investors, and offshore finance plays a role.

## International Relations

Antigua and Barbuda is a member of the Commonwealth, CARICOM, and the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States, with the British monarch as head of state. It has campaigned on climate change and reparations for slavery.
