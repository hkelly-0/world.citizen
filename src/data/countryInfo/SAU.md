## History

The Arabian Peninsula is the birthplace of Islam, founded by Muhammad in Mecca in the 600s. The House of Saud united most of the peninsula and founded the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932. Oil discovered in 1938 transformed the country. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman has led sweeping changes since 2017.

## People

Most citizens are Arab and Sunni Muslim, with a Shia minority in the east. About two-fifths of residents are foreign workers. Millions of Muslims visit Mecca and Medina each year for pilgrimage.

## Economy

Saudi Arabia is the world's largest oil exporter and the leading member of OPEC. Its Vision 2030 plan aims to diversify into tourism, entertainment, and technology.

## International Relations

Saudi Arabia is a regional power, a G20 member, and a long-standing US partner. It led a military intervention in Yemen from 2015 and is a rival of Iran.
