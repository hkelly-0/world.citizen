## History

Kuwait was a trading and pearling port ruled by the Al Sabah family from the 1700s, and a British protectorate from 1899. Oil was found in 1938, and it became independent in 1961. Iraq invaded in 1990 but was expelled by a US-led coalition in the 1991 Gulf War.

## People

Only about a third of residents are Kuwaiti citizens; the rest are foreign workers, mainly from South Asia, Egypt, and the Philippines. Most citizens are Sunni Muslim, with a large Shia minority.

## Economy

Kuwait has some of the world's largest oil reserves, which provide almost all its export income. Citizens receive generous benefits, and most work for the state.

## International Relations

Kuwait is a member of the Gulf Cooperation Council and a close US ally, hosting American troops. It has an elected parliament, unusually for the Gulf, though it was suspended in 2024.
