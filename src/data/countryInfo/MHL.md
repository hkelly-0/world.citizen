## History

The Marshall Islands were settled by Micronesians and later controlled by Germany and Japan, then the US after World War II. The US tested 67 nuclear weapons at Bikini and Enewetak atolls from 1946 to 1958, leaving lasting health effects and contamination. The Marshall Islands became independent in 1986 under a Compact of Free Association with the US.

## People

The country is made up of 29 coral atolls. Most people are Marshallese and Christian. Many have moved to the US, especially Arkansas.

## Economy

The economy relies on US aid, fishing licences, and one of the world's largest ship registries.

## International Relations

Under its compact, the US provides defence and runs a missile testing site at Kwajalein. The Marshall Islands recognizes Taiwan instead of China, and is threatened by rising seas.
