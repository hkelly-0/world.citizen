## History

The Marshall Islands were settled by Micronesians and later controlled by [Germany]{DEU} and [Japan]{JPN}, then the [US]{USA} after World War II. The US tested 67 nuclear weapons at Bikini and Enewetak atolls from 1946 to 1958, leaving lasting health effects and contamination. The Marshall Islands became independent in 1986 under a Compact of Free Association with the US.

## People

The country is made up of 29 coral atolls. Most people are Marshallese and Christian. Many have moved to the [US]{USA}, especially Arkansas.

## Economy

The economy relies on [US]{USA} aid, fishing licences, and one of the world's largest ship registries.

## International Relations

Under its compact, the [US]{USA} provides defence and runs a missile testing site at Kwajalein. The Marshall Islands recognizes [Taiwan]{TWN} instead of [China]{CHN}, and is threatened by rising seas.
