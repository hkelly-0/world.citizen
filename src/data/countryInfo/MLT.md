## History

Malta's location made it a prize for many powers, including Phoenicians, Romans, Arabs, and the Knights of St John, who held it from 1530. Britain ruled it from 1800, and it was heavily bombed in World War II. It became independent in 1964.

## People

Malta is the EU's smallest country and one of the most densely populated in the world. Maltese, a Semitic language written in the Latin alphabet, and English are both official. Most people are Catholic.

## Economy

Malta's economy is based on tourism, financial services, online gambling, and shipping. It has grown quickly in recent decades and attracts many foreign workers.

## International Relations

Malta joined the EU in 2004 and uses the euro. It is neutral and not in NATO. It is a member of the Commonwealth and an entry point for migrants crossing the Mediterranean.
