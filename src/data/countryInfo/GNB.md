## History

Guinea-Bissau was part of the Mali Empire, then a Portuguese colony and a centre of the slave trade. Led by Amílcar Cabral, it fought a war of independence and declared independence in 1973, recognized by Portugal in 1974. Since then it has had many coups and attempted coups, most recently in 2025.

## People

Guinea-Bissau has many ethnic groups, including the Balanta, Fula, and Mandinka. Islam, Christianity, and traditional religions are all practiced. Portuguese is official, and Kriol is widely spoken.

## Economy

Guinea-Bissau is one of the world's poorest countries. It relies heavily on cashew nuts, which make up most of its exports. It has been a transit route for cocaine to Europe.

## International Relations

Guinea-Bissau is a member of ECOWAS and the Community of Portuguese Language Countries.
