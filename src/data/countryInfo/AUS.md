## History

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have lived in Australia for over 60,000 years. Britain founded a penal colony in 1788, and colonization devastated Indigenous peoples. The colonies united in 1901 as the Commonwealth of Australia, and it fought alongside Britain in both World Wars.

## People

Most Australians live in coastal cities, especially Sydney and Melbourne. About 30% were born abroad, notably in England, India, and China. English is dominant, and many Indigenous languages survive.

## Economy

Australia has a wealthy economy, based on services and mining. It is a leading exporter of iron ore, coal, gas, and gold, mainly to China, and has strong education and tourism sectors.

## International Relations

Australia is a close US ally and part of the AUKUS pact with the US and UK. It is a member of the Commonwealth and G20, and China is its largest trading partner.
