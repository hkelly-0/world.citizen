## History

The land has deep religious significance for Jews, Christians, and Muslims. After centuries of Ottoman rule, it came under British control in 1917. Following the Holocaust, Israel was founded in 1948; in the war that followed, around 700,000 Palestinians fled or were expelled. Israel has occupied the West Bank and Gaza since 1967.

## People

About three-quarters of people are Jewish, many with roots in Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa, and about a fifth are Arab citizens. Hebrew is the official language, and Arabic has a special status.

## Economy

Israel has a wealthy, high-tech economy, known as the "Start-up Nation", with strengths in software, cybersecurity, defence, and pharmaceuticals.

## International Relations

The US is Israel's closest ally. It has peace treaties with Egypt, Jordan, and several Gulf states, but has fought many wars with its neighbours. Its war in Gaza since 2023 has drawn widespread international criticism.
