## History

Estonia was ruled for centuries by Germans, Danes, Swedes, and then Russia. It became independent in 1918, but was occupied by the Soviet Union in 1940, when many Estonians were deported. It regained independence in 1991.

## People

Most people are ethnic Estonians and speak Estonian, closely related to Finnish. About a quarter are ethnic Russians, mostly in Tallinn and the northeast. It is one of the least religious countries in the world.

## Economy

Estonia has a high-income economy and is a leader in digital government, allowing people to vote and do most official tasks online. It has produced many tech start-ups, including Skype.

## International Relations

Estonia joined the EU and NATO in 2004 and uses the euro. It strongly supports Ukraine and sees Russia as a major security threat.
