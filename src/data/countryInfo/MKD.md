## History

The region was part of ancient Macedonia and was ruled by the Ottomans for five centuries. It joined Yugoslavia and became independent peacefully in 1991. A dispute with Greece over its name ended in 2019, when it became North Macedonia.

## People

About two-thirds of people are ethnic Macedonians, mostly Orthodox Christians, and about a quarter are ethnic Albanians, mostly Muslim. Macedonian and Albanian are both official.

## Economy

North Macedonia's economy is based on services, manufacturing, and agriculture. Unemployment has fallen but remains high, and many young people emigrate.

## International Relations

North Macedonia joined NATO in 2020 and is an EU candidate, though Bulgaria has blocked progress over history and language disputes.
