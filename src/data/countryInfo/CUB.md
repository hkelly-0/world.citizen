## History

Cuba's Indigenous Taíno population collapsed after Spanish colonization began in 1511, and enslaved Africans were brought to work on sugar plantations. Cuba won independence in 1902 after a US intervention. Fidel Castro's revolution in 1959 created a communist state, which survived the end of its Soviet support in 1991.

## People

Cubans are of mixed Spanish and African descent. Spanish is the only official language. Education and healthcare are free, and literacy is nearly universal, but many people, especially the young, have emigrated in recent years.

## Economy

Cuba's economy is mostly state-run. Tourism, nickel, tobacco, and doctors sent abroad are major earners, along with money from relatives overseas. Shortages of food, fuel, and medicine are common, worsened by a long-standing US embargo.

## International Relations

Since 1960 the US has kept an economic embargo on Cuba, and the US naval base at Guantánamo Bay remains disputed. Cuba is allied with Venezuela, Nicaragua, Russia, and China, and has sent doctors to many developing countries.
