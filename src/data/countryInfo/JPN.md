## History

Japan was ruled by emperors and, for centuries, military shoguns, and isolated itself from 1639 until 1853. It then modernized rapidly and built an empire across East Asia. After its defeat in World War II, including atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, it became a pacifist democracy.

## People

Most people are ethnic Japanese and speak Japanese. The population is the world's oldest and is shrinking. Most people live in coastal cities, including Tokyo, the world's largest metropolitan area.

## Economy

Japan has one of the world's largest economies, known for cars, electronics, robotics, and machinery, with companies like Toyota and Sony. It has very high public debt and slow growth.

## International Relations

Japan is a close US ally and hosts many US troops. It belongs to the G7. Relations with China and the Koreas are affected by its wartime history and territorial disputes.
