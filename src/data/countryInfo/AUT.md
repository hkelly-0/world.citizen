## History

Austria was the centre of the Habsburg Empire, which ruled much of Central Europe for centuries. The empire collapsed after World War I. In 1938 Austria was annexed by Nazi Germany. After the war it was occupied by the Allies until 1955, when it became independent and permanently neutral.

## People

Most Austrians speak German and are Catholic, though many are non-religious. About a quarter of people have foreign roots. Vienna, home to around a fifth of the population, is famous for its music and coffee houses.

## Economy

Austria has a wealthy economy with strong manufacturing, especially machinery and metals, as well as tourism, particularly skiing in the Alps. Most of its electricity comes from hydropower.

## International Relations

Austria joined the EU in 1995 and uses the euro. It is neutral and not in NATO. Vienna hosts several UN agencies, OPEC, and the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe.
